# Reform and portfolio decision annex — I07

**The portfolio supports reform analysis, with applicability and performance findings still open.** Read the [versioned reform baseline](../data/reforms/baseline_release.json), [compliance view](reform_compliance_view.md) and [scoped agent review](reform_independent_review.md) together. This annex does not make a new legal determination, complete the session instrument population or verify operational compliance.

The selected baseline contains 65 extracted provision records: 33 classified as directions and 32 as requests. These are not 65 mandatory obligations applicable to every mission. Its declared assessment states include evidence missing and not yet due; it contains no verified compliant or noncompliant performance finding. The separate registers retain 48 ACABQ recommendations and 11 SG proposal or reported-arrangement records. Those records are neither additional adopted GA instructions nor evidence of implemented benefit.

| Authority or evidence layer | Permitted connection to portfolio decisions | Unresolved boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selected adopted GA80 provisions | Identify the exact paragraph, addressee, institutional competence, conditions and review timing relevant to a proposed audit or change | Complete instrument, revision and incorporated-advice coverage; case-specific applicability; actual delivery evidence |
| GA81 instruments | Record official index evidence and separately authenticate a potentially relevant adopted operative text | The bounded review extracted no authenticated in-scope action; this is not a finding that none exists or that earlier obligations lapse |
| ACABQ recommendations | Inform cost, control and evidence questions with their exact scope and proposal baseline | Advice alone does not appropriate funds, abolish a post, amend a mandate or prove Assembly endorsement |
| SG proposals and management reports | Describe proposed structures, resource estimates and attributed implementation accounts | Proposal publication is not intergovernmental approval; reported implementation requires claim-specific corroboration |
| Operational and financial records | Establish actual work, costs, quality, custody, settlement and outcomes at the required grain | Complete authorized records, comparable periods and owner acceptance are not available for substantive adjudication |

The [obligation crosswalk](../data/reforms/obligation_crosswalk.json) retains affected entities, functions, deadlines and conditions. Candidate text matches are research leads requiring substantive applicability review; they do not establish mandate authority, an appropriation relationship or financial attribution. Read [source authority](source_authority_standard.md) before converting a documentary link into a decision claim. Unknown deadlines are not overdue, and missing implementation evidence ordinarily supports `EVIDENCE_MISSING`, not noncompliance.

The priority cases operationalize reform questions without presuming an answer. RSCE asks whether the shared-service arrangement removes work and cost while preserving controls. UNIFIL–UNSCOL asks whether actual asset reuse meets readiness and substitutes for genuinely required procurement after transfer and residual costs. BINUH–UNSOH asks whether support changes lower total external cost and retained effort while preserving service. The remaining case packets cover specific controls, capabilities and transitions; their research inclusion does not supply institutional permission to alter them.

Each case must independently connect its proposed action to the applicable adopted provision or state that it is independently justified. Preserve Council mandate requirements alongside GA resource authority. Existing joint structures are part of the baseline; do not count already-shared work as a new consolidation benefit. Staffing abolishments, embedded proposal efficiencies and closure effects are baseline adjustments unless additionality is demonstrated. Recovery, capacity gain, cost avoidance and cashable saving remain distinct and nonadditive under the [cost-accounting standard](cost_accounting_standard.md).

Release advancement requires renewed official-source coverage, resolution of material authority/applicability gaps and authenticated outcome evidence. The current public baseline supports evidence preparation and review. It cannot certify reform compliance, operational validation, institutional approval or realized savings. Protecting mandate, safety, readiness, quality, timeliness and transition capability remains a condition of optimization rather than a financial offset.
