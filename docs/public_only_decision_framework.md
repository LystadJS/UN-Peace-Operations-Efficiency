## Tier 1 — Six immediately actionable documentary reviews

**P001 — UNIFIL–UNSCOL transfer.** Prepare the public source-to-use map for four light vehicles and two armored vehicles plus IT and rations. The $394.0k + $36.9k + $7.2k credits already sit in Add.4 pp.9–10. Security Council withdrawal and General Assembly separation, environmental and asset controls remain protected. Do not claim a completed property transfer without original asset identifiers and signed acceptance.

**P002 — BINUH–UNSOH services.** The Secretary-General reported the standard support handover effective 1 February 2026 (S/2026/325 para.40). Define the narrower aviation category, its booked $1.4m reduction, and the broader $4m service reimbursement provision. Public documents do not establish actual posted net payments or provider expenses. The $4m must not be subtracted mechanically from the $1.4m.

**P003 — RSCE financial boundaries.** Map the $3.2591m 2027 SPM allocation to RSCE client populations. The $45.9116m RSCE approved cost for 2026/27, and its mixed SPM and observer-entity share, are not the same annual cost object. Mark post abolition, new receiving positions, reassignments, and modeled FTE efficiencies separately.

**P004 — UNISFA translation centralization.** GA resolution 80/275 para.9 requests a translation performance and centralization review for 2027/28. Build a public alternative-service outline, retaining safety and observer mission requirements. No translation cost is independently isolated as avoidable.

**P005 — UNISFA underused/transferred assets.** GA resolution 80/275 paras.10–11 requires review of underused items and MINUSMA-transferred asset management. Establish evidence-to-decision checklists and ensure no premature asset disposal or unsafe fleet reduction.

**P006 — RSCE workforce model reconciliation.** The 2026/27 budget model reports -25 payroll-related transaction FTE, -10 other FTE, and +44 FTE for Kuwait clients (net +9 in that modeled workforce). A separate organization proposal describes 82 former client posts, 11 other proposed abolishments, 44 new transaction posts, and 17 reassigned RSCE/DOS payroll posts. These observations differ in counting unit, scope and stage. Maintain a unique-post crosswalk before any saving claim.

## Tier 2 — Existing resolutions and administrative arrangements

**P007** tracks UNSOS fuel contractor claims and the GA recovery follow-up (A/RES/80/285 para.4), without inventing a recoverable amount. **P008** tracks MONUSCO irregular medical-insurance claim controls (A/RES/80/278 para.9), without reducing medical readiness. **P009** follows implementation of the **91 already approved UNMISS civilian position abolishments**, including 53 electoral posts (A/RES/80/283 annex); these are not new savings. **P010** documents pre-existing co-location and shared leadership between UNOWAS and the Cameroon–Nigeria Mixed Commission; actual marginal shared-service costs remain unknown.

## Tier 3 — Four publicly testable product comparisons

**P011:** UNOWAS and UNOCA regional assessments, meetings, partner outreach, and shared cross-border threat analysis. **P012:** UNAMA and the Central Asian preventive-diplomacy centre's early-warning products. **P013:** Joint DPPA–DPO regional political-operational desks, distinguishing the full DPO support-account funding unit from the cost of any duplicate product. **P014:** Training course catalogs and schedules, controlling for separate audiences and readiness requirements.

The research question for each is **whether two entities incur avoidable, separable preparation or procurement work for the same authorized deliverable**. Similar language, geographic reach, training days, or stakeholders are insufficient evidence.

## Auditable release gates

[All 18 opportunities](../data/public_release_gates.csv) explicitly remain blocked from an executable financial cut. Requirements for further advancement:

1. Match the source, unit, time period, fund and approving authority.
2. Determine whether a proposed efficiency is already inside the approved or proposed baseline.
3. Preserve Security Council mandates, safety, human rights, confidential investigative independence and GA controls.
4. Verify unique eligible cost objects, vendor/post identity, counterfactual replacement costs, transition costs and residual obligations.
5. Only then calculate audited *incremental* costs and benefits; an unknown amount must remain **not estimated**, never zero.

The [base-R public board](../scripts/render_public_decision_board.R) generates a public-filtered review queue. The [conditional break-even model](../scripts/evaluate_conditional_options.R) accepts independently supplied eligible-cost inputs and calculates the avoided-share threshold and modeled present value. The included [synthetic fixture](../tests/fixtures/scenarios_SYNTHETIC.csv) contains **invented toy numbers explicitly tagged synthetic** for engineering verification only; no scenario output is evidence of UN savings.
