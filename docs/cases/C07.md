**BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE · SCREENING_CANDIDATE.** Retained evidence cutoff: 8 October 2026; assembled 23 October. This dossier does not refresh governing texts or grant operational authority.

The retained A/RES/80/278 paragraph 9 extraction requests stronger controls over irregular medical-insurance claims. Paragraphs 10–11 preserve mandate capability and medical-emergency readiness. No patient-level claims or adjudications are in the public corpus.

Test whether eligibility, duplicate-claim and reimbursement checks prevent unsupported payments without delaying legitimate care. A flag is not fraud and a repeated amount is not a duplicate without claim-level linkage. Owner-approved confidential claim adjudication and control redesign, with expedited clinical exceptions and appeal review; retain necessary care and emergency evacuation capacity.

Separate prevention, correction and recovery. Count only supported overpayments or avoided future payments against a valid counterfactual, net of administration and delayed-care costs. Provider, insurer and mission reimbursements can represent one episode; reconcile once. No medical claim exposure or recovery is estimated.

No live medical records should enter Git. Public derivatives must suppress patient identifiers and rare combinations that could reidentify individuals; the project has no access authorization.

Required safeguards:

- Timely medically necessary care and emergency evacuation
- Patient confidentiality, minimum necessary data and clinical independence
- Fair review, appeal and no automatic denial from algorithmic flags

Minimum evidence:

- Pseudonymized episode/claim IDs, eligibility dates and approved coverage rules
- Provider invoice, service date, procedure category, authorization and insurer adjudication
- Payment, reversal, reimbursement and recovery links with appeal outcome
- Control timestamps, care-delay indicators, exception handling and independent clinical review
