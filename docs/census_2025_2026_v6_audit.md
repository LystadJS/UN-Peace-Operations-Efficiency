# Cross-version census audit: identities, course delivery and source reuse

**As of 2026-10-15 | Public evidence | Frozen financial and mandate controls unchanged**

## Decision

**The bounded cross-version identity audit is complete.** The 13 curated CSV registers/views contain 371 observations, 130 local IDs and **123 canonical evidence identities**, after reconciling five alias clusters. These mixed-grain identities are not 123 delivered events. All 8,385 ID pairs were screened; 191 candidate pairs were reviewed, with nine positive pair links absorbing seven aliases. The source-URL frame and the unobserved operational population are not being declared complete.

The eight course listings were checked, but **no exact-cohort completion is newly certified**: two scheduled periods have elapsed, one spans the cutoff and five begin in the future. This is neither a cancellation finding nor an assertion that the elapsed courses did not occur.

The source audit authenticated **40 PDFs: 33 requested report files and seven transcripts**. One report file is an identified revision. Seven other transcripts remain unavailable. The report comparisons establish six cross-mandate referral pairs and two references to previous reporting periods; six other pairs have no explicit paired-document reference in the extracted text.

## Shared-production recommendations

1. **Joint regional evidence packet:** Use canonical IDs for the Nigeria visit, Governors Forum and Dakar workshop, with source-specific dates, roles and approved facts. Retain distinct UNOWAS and UNOCA analysis and Council products. This builds on explicit source referral and briefing reuse already observed.
2. **Cyprus and Lebanon reference controls:** Maintain a common source catalogue and cross-reference/version checks rather than merging good-offices, peacekeeping or resolution-specific reports. Test whether existing referrals avoid duplicate evidence requests; actual drafting effort is not measured here.
3. **Time-stamped report-to-briefing updates:** Keep shared factual inputs, their original cutoff and subsequent amendments distinct. Afghanistan returnee totals changed between reporting dates; a later briefing must not inherit a stale total merely for consistency.
4. **Course administration, not inferred delivery:** Link stable course/cohort IDs to existing ITS/LCM systems, with advertised, opened, completed and certified statuses separated. Related topics, shared articles and equal headcounts are not grounds for merging specialist instruction.

## Quality controls and limits

**Nigeria date discrepancy:** S/2025/187 paragraph 49 records 28-31 January; S/2025/342 paragraph 61 records 27-31 January. Both ranges remain attached to one identity. The reason for the different start dates is not established.

**Source access:** Two press-site responses were client challenges despite successful HTTP retrieval; they are not article bodies. Original transcripts take precedence over inconsistent press metadata. Low-text/scanned annexes are listed and not certified by text extraction. Original PDF bytes were SHA-256 checked and re-extracted; structured offline checks alone do not repeat this authentication.

**Preserved:** All frozen pre-V6 source tables, mandated products, parent/mission rankings and 20/32 previous retrospective output-category support. **Not established:** a complete operational activity denominator, cross-course unique people, duplicate paid services or incremental net savings.
