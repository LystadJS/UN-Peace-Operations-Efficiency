# 2025–2026 training attendance and field-activity census extension

**Source cutoff 13 October 2026 | Public UN evidence | Mission, UNITAR, UNSSC and UN Peacekeeping archives**

## Bottom line

**Thirty official-source attendance observations have been reconciled to existing or newly assigned activity identities.** They comprise **22 previously unrecorded activity or programme-group identifiers** and **eight attendance enrichments of existing EV/CJ/XEV records**. Of the 22 new identities, **three represent multi-course or multi-month programmes**, not single events; 19 have individual activity/course grain. The source publications report **22 numeric attendance counts, four approximate counts and four combined/ambiguous counts**. No individual rosters, mutually exclusive people denominator, duplicate paid service or incremental net financial savings have been established.

The census is **systematic at the bounded public source-index level, not an exhaustive census of delivered training**. [Full attendance claims](../data/census_v4/attendance_evidence.csv), [normalized participant count controls](../data/census_v4/attendance_normalized_controls.csv), [identity crosswalk](../data/census_v4/reconciled_event_identity.csv).

## Deeper official archive and calendar frame

An eight-page bounded scan of **11 official archive/calendar sources** retrieved **79/88 listing pages**, yielding **767 distinct index links**. MONUSCO's general `/en/actualites` archive returned 404 for all eight attempts, although its individually published reports and other official UN Peacekeeping indexes remained accessible. The Climate Security Mechanism returned seven of eight pages. The newer index adds **530 exact URL identities** after reconciling against the October 12 1,708-link and October 13 first-round 249-link frames. Their combined exact URL inventory contains **2,487** links.

| Measure | Source-frame result |
|---|---:|
| Earlier October 12 indexed notice URLs | 1,708 |
| Initial October 13 nine-source index URLs | 249 (no exact overlap with October 12) |
| Deeper 11-source index URLs | 767 |
| URLs already represented in previous frames | 237 |
| **Deduplicated URLs across all three frames** | **2,487** |
| Automatically certified unique events from index cards | **0** |

The deeper scan is **not necessarily exhaustive** (eight-page ceiling, variable site pagination, dead URLs and incomplete official publication). It neither retrieved article bodies for every index entry nor established a denominator of all 2025–2026 real mission visits or training sessions.
