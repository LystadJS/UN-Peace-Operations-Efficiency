# C01 — Cutover evidence request addendum

**Draft only, 23 October 2026. Not sent; collection authority and accepted ownership are absent.** The [field-level addendum](../../data/priority_cases/C01_request_addendum.json) preserves the original [E01 contract](../../data/evidence_requests/E01_rsce.json). It requests no actual record through this public repository.

The [public evidence refresh](C01_public_evidence.md) located official indexed reporting of an April client commencement and a 1 July formal function handover. These are different reported milestones whose client/service coverage remains unresolved. Neither date establishes an observation window or proves predecessor charging ceased. Before selecting a baseline, the custodian must identify one eligible client and service line and supply a complete, dated coverage bridge.

The addendum defines three purpose-bound declarations:

| Bridge | Decision uncertainty resolved |
| --- | --- |
| Client/service/phase coverage | Which exact tasks and clients commenced in pilot, transition and formal handover phases, under what effective-date authority? |
| Predecessor/provider/client accounting | Which actual service event and period does each gross charge, credit, reversal, settlement or trailing adjustment represent? What evidence identifies predecessor closeout and provider commencement? |
| Delegation and retained work | Which client tasks are required delegated approvals or controls, which processing moved to RSCE or DOS, and what work actually remained? |

Stable organizational and economic-event tokens link these bridges to E01's charges and workload observations. Keys and field purposes are explicit. Actual records, tokens and re-identification maps remain in approved restricted systems; no personal or sensitive narrative fields are requested. Missing effective dates, last charges or handover acceptance remain missing, rather than being treated as zero expenditure, full coverage or proof of duplication.

The case slice, client, service line, baseline/intervention dates, posting lag, burden and numerical safeguards remain unset. A competent accepted owner must approve purpose, permissible fields, completeness, comparable periods, burden and handling before dispatch or collection. The original service safeguards remain binding.

An overlapping charge period can represent accrual timing, split services, trailing closeout or a necessary residual control. It is a reconciliation question. Matched internal transfers must be eliminated once across client, predecessor, RSCE and DOS perimeters; retained work and receiving-entity external costs remain included. No monetary benefit is estimated and the candidate remains a screening case.
