# Executive readout — 2025–2026 UN peace-operations activity census

**8 October 2026 | Scope: official published sources, not confidential transaction records**

**Finding:** A bounded, reproducible scan of the official Security Council annual SG report indexes identifies **79 unique reports in 17 selected peace-operations series** (46 in 2025; 33 through 8 October 2026). The supplementary public activity collection contains **49 entries**, including a 2024 event preserved as a temporal negative control. The reports are systematically indexed for the selected series, while **meetings, field activities and training are not yet exhaustively covered**.

**Actionable shared work:** Use a single canonical source/event record for joint UNOWAS–UNOCA activities cited by distinct regional reports. UNRCCA and UNAMA delivered a joint Academy session on 28 April 2026, supporting shared course administration. The April 2026 Sierra Leone joint assessment involved UNOWAS, ECOWAS and the Commonwealth, not UNOCA. A former UNAMA employee lecturing at an UNRCCA event is not evidence of UNAMA co-organization.

**Preserve distinct mandates:** 14 report-pair tests distinguish shared political geography or publication dates from separate reports under Cyprus, Lebanon, DRC, UNOWAS and UNOCA mandates. No pair demonstrates duplicate financing or proves identical full-text reporting.

**Research limitation:** This is not a complete 2025–2026 census of UN Council meetings, mission field activities or training. Comprehensive collection will require S/PV meeting registers and mission course/event calendars. No verified additional net savings.
