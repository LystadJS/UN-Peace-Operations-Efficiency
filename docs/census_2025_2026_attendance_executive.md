# Executive decision brief — verified training and field participation

**13 October 2026 | UN Peace Operations Efficiency | Public-document evidence only**

## Decision

**Prioritize shared training governance, procurement administration and versioned learning materials—not elimination of mandated courses or reporting.** Deeper mission archives and UNITAR/UNSSC sources confirm additional 2025–2026 field training, published attendance, co-financing and source-reuse opportunities. **No separately paid duplicate service or additional net saving is verified.**

### Important source corrections

The previously undated UNSSC–DPO gender/WPS programme for **60 personnel** took place in **August 2022** and is **excluded** from the 2025–2026 event population. By contrast, the 27-person DPO analytics boot camp has verifiable February–March **2025** dates.

UNITAR's Quarter II 2025 report displays **15 training events and 504 trainees** across its broader Division for Peace, but a graphic subcaption refers to “Quarter I”; it is retained as a **period-caption discrepancy**, not a verified 2025 Q2 peacekeeping-only denominator. It also explicitly distinguishes DPO standards from UNITAR pedagogical support.

**Budget protection:** MONUSCO's reported **$1.2 million** funding for Muda Wa Amani II is for a broader reintegration project including 200 vocational completers, **not the incurred cost of their training** and not savings. No course advertisement, attendance claim or grant may be subtracted from a mission budget without audited allocation data.
