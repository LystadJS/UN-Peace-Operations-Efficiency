# Independent agent review of reform authority — R17

**The scoped reform baseline is suitable for further research with its declared gaps; it is not a complete legal or compliance baseline.** Review performed on 23 October 2026 by a separate analysis agent from the R03/R04 authors. This is project review, not independent institutional assurance, legal counsel approval, owner acceptance or UN authorization. No implementation or financial-validation evidence was reviewed.

The review examined the R03–R06 instrument/action registers and their explanatory documents, reproduced selected official-source retrievals through the web service, compared selected operative provisions and adoption receipts, and ran the obligation validator against both action registers. Both validation commands passed. Passing checks establish internal declaration consistency, not legal interpretation or population completeness.

The official [A/RES/80/251 final PDF](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=A%2FRES%2F80%2F251&t=pdf) independently rendered as six pages. Its header and terminal record support adoption on 31 March 2026 at the 76th plenary meeting. Selected checks covered mandate validity and stock-review exclusions in OP20–25, the distinct review deadlines in OP16/18/24 and OP33, and human oversight for AI tools in OP29. These support the baseline's restrictions on treating mandate review as permission to remove ongoing operations. OP13 requires attention to new and renewing mandates; the three-to-five-year cycle language is encouragement, not a mandatory interval. This review did not independently adjudicate every extracted provision.

The official [A/RES/80/242 final PDF](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=A%2FRES%2F80%2F242&t=pdf) independently rendered as fifteen pages, adopted on 30 December 2025. Its explicit partial coverage, unresolved action-specific authority and empty-extraction limitation are appropriate: no qualifying extracted obligation is different from no applicable obligation.

Two qualifier improvements were referred to the GA80 author: preserve the OP13 scope/cycle distinction and the OP86 reporting-time basis. These are interpretation safeguards; they do not replace the controlling text. The release must retain them either in the records or as this explicit review qualification.

Material residual review gates remain: mission-financing final-text re-verification; complete session instrument and amendment/corrigendum coverage; paragraph-specific tracing of incorporated advice; affected-entity and competent-authority applicability; and authenticated delivery evidence before any compliance conclusion. No raw official PDF bytes were retained during this review, so no new primary-file checksum is asserted. No additional owner request was sent and no live-use gate was changed.

A complete downstream decision must resolve the relevant gaps for its own action and preserve the distinction between adopted direction, advisory recommendation, proposal, implementation evidence and verified outcome. This review supports publication as a **partial public research baseline**, with these limitations carried into current status and decision materials.
