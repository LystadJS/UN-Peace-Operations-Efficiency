# Consolidation decision brief — V9 Cyprus transfer test

**15 October 2026 | Additive to V8 | Mandates, frozen rankings and financial restrictions unchanged**

## Recommendation

**Use the Cyprus case to test source/version controls within existing good-offices–UNFICYP arrangements, not to create another coordinating unit or merge reports.** The V8 model extends to the selected Cyprus reporting pairs as a way of making factual reuse and differences inspectable. It does not show that either office performs avoidable duplicate preparation. [Full transfer assessment](v9_cyprus_transfer_assessment.md).

Eight reports across four cycles yield five directed companion-report links and 15 citation occurrences. Existing cooperation is explicit: the Youth Technical Committee's inaugural meeting was co-facilitated, the committees operate under good-offices auspices with UNFICYP support, and the Special Representative is identified as also serving as Deputy Special Adviser in the July 2025 report. All are **baseline arrangements**, not new efficiencies to book. [Sources and claims](../data/census_v9/claim_ledger.csv).

## What adds decision value

| Both July 2025 reports print EUR15.2m Green Line trade, but their export comparators are EUR4.12bn and EUR2.14bn. | Keep values attributed; flag definition/vintage conflict; obtain underlying statistical basis before using one ratio. | Which figure is wrong, a silently corrected common denominator, or an observed error-rate reduction. |

| Annexes carry leaders' written submissions. | Preserve party attribution and separate it from Secretariat findings. | UN endorsement merely because a statement appears within an official report. |

## Decision sequence

**Existing-workflow comparison → narrowly scoped shadow test → authorized substitution test → cost-object verification.**

The immediate owner-review question is whether the present joint process already preserves these dates, definitions, statuses and attributions. Where it does, retain it and close the duplicate proposal. Where a specific gap exists, test the lightweight metadata control in approved storage. Good offices and UNFICYP retain separate analytical responsibility and approvals; committee/project implementation data stay with the relevant existing custodian.

Record factual fidelity, qualification loss, update timing and all added maintenance. Do not call parallel shadow effort avoided labor. Only after a named task can actually be replaced without concealed transfer or reduced quality should eligible cancelable costs and transition/operating costs be assessed. No financial term is populated in V9.

## Completion and limits

The retrospective prototype contains **15 components, 41 observations and eight separate report views**. **48 source/representation checks pass, including rejection of 18 deliberate corruptions.** Original files and earlier data are preserved. The model is technically representable in a second selected context. Live owner approval, measured operational benefit, cross-report labor substitution, cash savings, and transfer to Cyprus oral briefings remain unverified. The paused course watch was not executed or re-enabled.
