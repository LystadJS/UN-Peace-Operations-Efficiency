# UNGA81 reform baseline — R04 and R06

**No in-scope mandatory GA81 reform action was authenticated in this bounded review through 23 October 2026.** This is an evidence limitation, not a finding that no obligation exists. Session 81 remains open, and the review does not certify its complete decisions population.

The [instrument register](../data/reforms/unga81_instruments.json) records the five resolution rows visible in the [UN library's session table](https://research.un.org/en/docs/ga/quick/regular/81), including the table linked directly by that page. Those rows are A/RES/81/1–5. Their index titles concern participation by Palestine, Article 19 requests, sea-level rise, the Durban declaration and pandemic preparedness. The index supplies adoption dates and meeting-record references; final operative texts were not retrieved and no peacekeeping efficiency instruction is inferred from their titles.

The [Fifth Committee outcome index](https://www.un.org/en/ga/fifth/81/resdec81.shtml), available through a search-rendered excerpt while direct retrieval returned 403, lists A/RES/81/2 for Article 19 requests. Its 2027 programme-budget and administrative-efficiency outcome rows were blank in the retrieved excerpt. Blank rows and unsuccessful searches do not establish non-adoption. The [President's 8 October statement](https://www.un.org/pga/81/documents/speeches/general-assembly-revitalization-joint-debate-8-october-2026/) describes continuing revitalization and mandate-review work; it is not substituted for an adopted resolution.

The [mandatory-action register](../data/reforms/unga81_actions.json) therefore contains an empty `obligations` list with an explicit evidence gate. Empty means no qualifying action extracted, not zero obligations, compliance, implementation or permission to reduce services. A/81/6 programme-budget proposals and A/81/7 ACABQ advice retain their separate proposal and advisory classifications. Their session symbols do not establish GA approval.

Before asserting a GA81 requirement, retrieve the final adopted text and adoption record, identify its operative paragraph and addressee, determine competence and conditions, and map it to the affected function and period under the [source-authority standard](source_authority_standard.md). Refresh both resolution and decision coverage at each release, including later instruments changing GA80 requirements. Source rendering was inspected on 23 October 2026; raw source bytes were not retained, so no file checksum or full-text authentication is claimed.
