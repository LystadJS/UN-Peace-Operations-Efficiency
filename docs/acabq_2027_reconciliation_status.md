# 2027 ACABQ recommendations: mission-level and cluster-level reconciliation

**UN Peace Operations Efficiency | 8 October 2026 | Primary-source audit**

## Executive result

**The uploaded ACABQ reports permit full numerical reconciliation of 27 of the 36 continuing 2027 special political missions.** The 12 missions in **Cluster I** receive a **net advisory reduction of $182,500** from the Secretary-General's proposed $56.3312 million; the 15 missions in **Cluster II** are recommended for approval without a further quantified adjustment to their $37.7268 million proposal. The resulting **reviewed two-cluster subtotal** is **$93.8755 million**, compared with $94.0580 million submitted by the Secretary-General.

**No complete ACABQ-adjusted total for the original $422.6710 million SPM request can be stated.** The user supplied A/81/7, A/81/7/Add.2 and A/81/7/Add.3. The ACABQ cluster III report **A/81/7/Add.4** and the cross-cutting SPM report **A/81/7/Add.1** are referred to inside the uploaded reports, but their bodies are not included and the current public index did not independently verify their issuance. Nine Cluster III mission amounts and the separate RSCE allocation consequently remain *not reviewed*, **not zero**.

### Staffing-versus-cost distinctions

- **Geneva discussions (para. 20):** The ACABQ recommends treating one proposed Administrative Assistant (General Service) post as **general temporary assistance for 2027**, with **no stated change to the funding proposal**. It expects a future test of whether existing New York/Geneva and common administrative service capacities can sustain the workload.
- **Syria (paras. 11, 21 and 31):** The committee opposes the proposed Chief of Staff downward reclassification *for now*, pending wider review; it recommends phasing the **$2.4337 million nonrecurrent relocation preparation**, yielding the **$121,700** decrease. Separate potential changes in Geneva rent and Damascus accommodation **are contingent and not an estimate of net relocation savings**.
- **Yemen (paras. 23 and 32):** UNMHA's 2026 wind-down and transferred capacities are already in the SG baseline. The Committee supports reviewing staff and backstopping once liquidation stabilizes. Its **$90,100** aviation adjustment is a proposed 2027 spending reduction, *not independent evidence of duplicated flight service*.

## Cluster II — all 15 missions numerically unadjusted, with material oversight conditions

The ACABQ recommends the SG's **99 positions** and **$18.6523 million proposed operational budget** for approval (A/81/7/Add.3 paras. 19 and 24); **none of the 15 mission proposals receives a new quantified reduction in this report**. Cluster II remains **$37.7268 million**.

However, the report describes **239.1 expert-months budgeted but not deployed in 2025**, with liquidity, delayed appointments, visas and security constraints contributing (paras. 12–13 and 22). This is **not automatically avoidable funding**: the Committee explicitly asks for more accurate deployment-based budgeting *while preserving mandate implementation*. The Monitoring Team, with eight experts and 16 supporting staff, is identified for workload and vacancy-repurposing review (para. 17), not ordered cut.

The Committee records that Member States declined a proposal to combine counterterrorism bodies and emphasized that **CTED's independent analytical and assessment function remain separate** from UNOCT policy/capacity-building and UNODC technical assistance (para. 5). This is a **substantive legal/mandate negative control** in the project's consolidation ranking.

## Cross-cutting implications for the efficiency project

The main report A/81/7 directly supports the research agenda:

1. **No verified Secretariat-wide savings ledger:** Chapter I para. 10 observes that the Secretariat lacks a centralized registry and consistent method to quantify realized efficiencies. It recommends a consolidated reporting framework identifying implementation, costs, productivity and savings and distinguishing structural gains from cash shortages.
2. **Shared services and CAPs:** Chapter I paras. 79–93 calls for a common baseline of mandatory administrative services, comparable service-provider costs, transparent exceptions, and performance indicators. Delegated substantive decisions should remain under accountable heads of entities.
3. **Training:** Chapter I paras. 68–69 notes that the Secretariat lacks activity-based training costing and encourages consistent expenditure and results tracking and the reduction of fragmentation. It does **not** identify DPPA and DPO course-level duplicate expenses.
4. **DPPA regional divisions and envoys:** Part II, Section 3 paras. II.4–II.7 requests holistic review of DPPA regional offices, liaison presences and special envoys and stronger DPPA–DPO cooperation, including use of established field capacities.
5. **Already authorized reforms:** Prior headquarters, SPM and UN80 staff reorganizations should be treated as the 2026 baseline. Staff not filled because of the liquidity crisis, and budgets for previously mandated closures, **are not independently realized productivity gains**.

See [20 recommendation-linked implementation directives](../data/acabq_2027_qualitative_directives.csv).

## Important provenance and disagreements retained

- **Main report A/81/7, Section 3 paragraph II.2** repeats the earlier provisional **$423.5445m** SPM 2027 proposal, whereas the current Add.1 summary and **A/81/7 Chapter I Table 1** use **$422.6710m**. The **$0.8735m** difference is a **version/control-total discrepancy**, not an additional ACABQ cut. The current detailed SPM chapeau is the financial source of truth.
- **Main report A/81/7 Table 9** reports 2027 SPM extrabudgetary resources of **$32.9772m**, versus **$32.976673m** obtained from the detailed previously extracted voluntary and cost-recovery items. The **$527** nominal difference remains a recorded minor source-precision/scope discrepancy, not invented revenue or duplicated charge.
- **Main report A/81/7** cannot substitute for the mission-specific **A/81/7/Add.4** advisory recommendations or the cross-cutting **A/81/7/Add.1**. These were not uploaded. Cluster III, RSCE and complete SPM recommendations remain **not determined**.
- **No GA 2027 appropriation** is documented in the supplied ACABQ advisory reports. The finance status must be `recommended_not_adopted` throughout this analysis.

All uploaded original PDFs are tracked by filename, page count, byte size and SHA-256 in [ACABQ source manifest](../data/acabq_2027_source_manifest.csv). Source reports themselves remain referenced using official UN document symbols rather than replicated into the public repository.
