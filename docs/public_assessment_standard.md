# Public assessment standard

**Primary deliverable and acceptance standard | 23 October 2026**

The project will publish a defensible public-evidence assessment of specific peacekeeping efficiency concerns and applicable reform-accountability questions. Its value is a qualified answer and practical next decision, including an honest unknown or a supported choice to retain an arrangement. It has no positive savings target. This standard controls the primary route under the [desired-end-state amendment](desired_end_state.md); it does not declare the assessment complete.

## Frozen scope and denominators

[PUBLIC-PRIORITY-2026-10-23](../data/public_assessment/scope.json) freezes the evidence cutoff at 23 October 2026. C01 RSCE, C02 UNIFIL–UNSCOL assets and C03 Haiti support receive detailed public assessments. All fourteen historical parent cases remain visible in the triage denominator. C04–C14 are explicitly retained screening entries outside this release's detailed assessment, with labels, reasons and reopening triggers. Report detailed coverage as three of fourteen, separately from triage coverage and source coverage; neither means operational audit coverage. Revised inclusion, cutoff or questions require a successor version and an explanation, preserving difficult and adverse cases.

Each detailed case must define the decision question, entities, function, relevant period, financial perimeter and source limits. Public reports about commencement, plans or transfers are distinguished from actual service observations and transaction receipts. The proposed UF01 research child and its F03 readiness history remain a separate record.

## Public dispositions

These dispositions describe public-evidence conclusions. They are separate from F03 candidate advancement states, historical BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE labels and institutional decisions.

| Disposition | Required meaning and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| SUPPORTED_CONCERN | A bounded concern is supported by directly relevant, qualified public evidence. State the demonstrated mechanism, period and limits; historical findings do not establish continuing present waste. The disposition alone establishes neither quantified waste nor a financial benefit. |
| CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITY | Evidence supports a plausible specific improvement mechanism, with the necessary feasibility, authority, service or financial conditions named. A proposal or embedded efficiency is not observed performance or an incremental benefit. |
| UNRESOLVED_QUESTION | Available public evidence cannot distinguish materially different explanations or establish the asserted mechanism. Identify the exact unknown and the evidence required; absence of evidence is not evidence of waste, noncompliance or effective performance. |
| RETAIN_CURRENT_PROCESS | Evidence supports retaining a bounded current arrangement, or rejecting the particular proposed change, with reasons, alternatives and reassessment trigger. Insufficient information alone warrants an unresolved question rather than an affirmative effectiveness finding. |

Use a disposition for each material decision question and an explained overall case synthesis. Mixed subquestions may receive different dispositions; the synthesis must preserve them. Do not convert a supported historical concern into a current finding without current evidence. Each conclusion cites exact source receipts and distinguishes fact, interpretation and recommendation. Record contrary evidence and feasible competing explanations.

## Authority and financial treatment

Apply F04 to each source: adopted GA decisions and resolutions, ACABQ advice, SG proposals, management reports and independent oversight have distinct effects. Council mandates and specific administrative competence remain distinct from budget approval. Registry transcriptions and search-index excerpts carry retrieval limitations; raw-source verification is not inferred from a stored URL or hash. Source date, retrieval date, period covered and final-text access status must be explicit. Selected GA80/GA81 evidence is a bounded population: no blanket compliance, noncompliance or completed session-81 adoption claim follows from symbol numbering or missing implementation evidence.

Classify each monetary reference by currency, unit, period, entity/fund perimeter, source authority and proposal/adopted/actual/validated status. Keep embedded proposal effects, recoveries, cost avoidance, capacity gain, cashable saving and realized saving distinct under F02/F05. Do not add internal charges, overlapping cases, shared transition costs or prebooked efficiencies as incremental benefits. Unknown is different from measured zero. Public concern or opportunity dispositions never authorize calling an unvalidated monetary finding “savings”; FINANCIALLY_VALIDATED and the applicable evidence requirements still govern that language.

## Completion and acceptance

The machine-readable scope records PA01–PA09. Acceptance requires all of the following, or a precise explicit inapplicability receipt for a genuinely irrelevant item:

1. Freeze questions, cutoff, three detailed cases and fourteen visible triage entries, with transparent exclusions.
2. Answer each detailed question with source-linked qualified reasoning, contrary evidence, an explicit disposition and limitations. An unresolved answer can be complete as public analysis when feasible public checks have been performed and residual uncertainty is precise.
3. Separate source authority, financial classes and actual versus proposed or reported events. Disclose the bounded reform population and unresolved searches.
4. Have a reviewer other than the case author review every material conclusion, its sources, alternatives and accounting. Bind scope and case versions or hashes. Name agent review as agent review; it is not independent UN, legal or financial certification. Missing review or unresolved material disagreement cannot silently count as a pass.
5. Reproduce the case outputs, triage and leadership brief from versioned public records with source receipts and checks. Passing software tests is not substantive review.
6. Resolve feasible public-research gaps through documented searches and source comparison. For unavailable sources or unresolved contradictions, identify what was attempted, the exact blocked conclusion, and the next public source or optional authorized operational evidence. Do not require inaccessible internal records merely to publish an honest public unknown.
7. Record actionable next decisions, proposed responsible roles and reopening triggers. New adopted instruments, material later evidence, amended authority, corrected source text, changed scope or authorized operational access trigger reassessment rather than silent overwriting.
8. Release with zero unsupported savings claims, known double-counted amounts, restricted records disclosed or unresolved critical authority/current-status contradictions.

Report acceptance against these public criteria separately from retained operational gates. Publication is allowed before completion when clearly labeled draft or partial, with outstanding criteria listed. Completion requires an explicit review receipt against the frozen scope, not a declaration that old operational gates have passed.

## Optional operational continuation

Authorized access can reopen or deepen any question through F03 and the retained operational path in F01. Accepted owners, actual comparable baselines, service safeguards, financial adjudication and implementation authority remain required at their applicable gates. None is invented by public-assessment completion. No public assessment authorizes dispatching requests, collecting restricted records, releasing assets or changing operations. Operational and financial audit counts, benefits and parent states remain their actual recorded values.
