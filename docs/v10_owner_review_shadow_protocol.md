# V10 — Joint owner-review and shadow-cycle protocol

**UN Peace Operations Efficiency | 15 October 2026 | Version 10.0**  
**Status: prepared for owner review; not institutional authorization. No live trial, staff measurement, course check or automated task has been started.**

## Decision and scope

Test whether a lightweight source/version record adds value **beyond the jointly supported workflows that already exist**. Preserve separate political assessments, operational responsibilities, mandated reports, briefings, deadlines and approvals. Do not establish a new drafting unit, platform, staff assignment or financial target through this protocol.

The two cases are **SP08P1: UNOWAS–UNOCA** and **SP09P1: Cyprus good offices–UNFICYP**. They share an evaluation method, not a clearance chain. Each case can be accepted, revised, stopped or declined independently. An office's silence is not approval. The project repository is the record of this public research design; it is **not** the destination for internal drafts, personnel logs or sensitive source material.

**Recommendation:** first compare the prototype with each case's actual existing process. Close the proposed addition where equivalent controls already operate. Only a documented gap warrants an authorized, non-displacing shadow test. A positive shadow result can justify designing a later substitution test; it cannot establish avoided labor or cash savings.

Baseline: repository commit `65fa21bfd2605c0372f3626deb25f69d0fee1db4`. V10 is additive: its generator copies identifiers, source locators, values and classifications from the retained V8/V9 designs without editing them.

| V9 Cyprus | 15 | 41 | 41 | 8 |
| Combined inventory only | **25** | **64** | **61** | **12** |

**Counting rule:** an observation is an attributed source assertion, potentially containing several qualifications—not an independent event, sentence, person or treatment unit. A review obligation is one selected observation in its own published product. V8's three notice observations support the products but are not three additional output obligations. Source information can appear in several support views without increasing the fixed 61-obligation denominator. Do not pool these counts into a statistical sample-size claim.

The immutable reference files are [fixed source scope](../data/census_v10/fixed_source_scope.csv), [product obligations](../data/census_v10/product_obligations.csv), [product register](../data/census_v10/product_scope.csv), [component boundaries](../data/census_v10/component_scope.csv) and [input hashes](../data/census_v10/input_manifest.json). IDs are namespaced (`V8:O01`, `V9:CY001`) to avoid confusing V8 product R1 with other versions' identifiers.

**Preserved controls.** Include office-specific analysis, CNMC boundaries, 2024 contextual material, the revised January Cyprus report, party-submission attribution, implementation stages, mismatched count units and financial denominators. These are not all candidates for shared drafting. A required boundary-preservation action is scored as such; it is not excluded because it cannot produce a saving. No new Cyprus oral-briefing claim is included.

**Source truth is qualified.** The reference ledger is the research team's reviewed evidence, not institutional or independent-human gold. Owners review original cited sources and may identify errors. Record a proposed correction in an amendment; retain the original and stop affected scoring until resolved. Do not silently overwrite a source disagreement or historical snapshot.

## 2. Fix the live population before evaluating outcomes

The 61 historical obligations are fixed now. They do **not** identify the still-unknown claims in a future live reporting cycle. The live gate therefore requires a separate prospective manifest, rather than pretending future facts have been frozen.

**Default first live window, subject to owner acceptance:** for SP08P1, one linked cycle containing each office's selected report and its following briefing; for SP09P1, one same-cycle pair of written reports. Record the exact target product identifiers and existing deadlines before activation. Windows need not begin together, and no office changes its timetable to fit the pilot. This small, selected exercise is a feasibility study, not an impact or non-inferiority trial.

Freeze the component-family eligibility rule, product list, approved information cutoff, language, task requirements, source access and reviewer rubric before either method's output is assessed. Enroll **every** eligible assertion or required withholding/referral action in those families, including disagreements, low-quality inputs and failed tasks. Predetermined exclusions are confidential/unauthorized inputs, out-of-scope products, actual diplomacy/field operations and other component families. Log excluded candidates with a non-sensitive reason; do not use eventual performance to decide eligibility.

When facts arrive after the initial freeze, create a numbered **matched update wave**. Append its observation IDs, source hashes, availability timestamps, qualified values and receiving-product obligations before measuring that wave. Give both methods the same newly authorized inputs at the same logged point. Keep the earlier wave immutable. Late facts required for normal production are never withheld to protect the experiment: normal production proceeds and the comparison is marked non-comparable or rebaselined in a new wave.

Every enrolled obligation receives a disposition: fulfilled, required boundary preserved, failed, pending, excluded by a documented pre-existing rule, or withdrawn following a recorded incident. Failed, pending and stopped items remain in the enrolled denominator. New exclusions or scope changes require a new signed version and an all-enrolled sensitivity account; no favorable-only denominator is permitted.

## 3. Owner review and activation

Use the [owner decision sheet](v10_owner_decision_sheet.md). Do not send it to offices or invite participants without a separate instruction.

| Responsibility | Existing role to be nominated locally | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Case sponsorship and permission | Authorized representatives of **both** workflows in the case | Written purpose, permission and stop authority; participation is not a transfer of mandate. |
| Each final product | Its existing substantive owner and clearance authority | Confirm untouched analysis, clearance, deadline and fallback. |
| Shared factual record | A custodian agreed by both owners, within existing arrangements | Access boundary, update responsibility, conflict escalation and maintenance budget. |
| Assessment | Reviewer separate from the preparer where feasible | Rubric, conflicts-of-interest and prior-exposure declaration; no unsupported claim of independence. |
| Information protection | Relevant local records/security/privacy authority | Approved storage, access, retention/deletion schedule and incident route. |
| Workload accounting | Named local measurement custodian | Consistent timer rules, expected-task reconciliation, limited-purpose staff notice and no personnel ranking. |

Before live start, the case-local activation record must contain: authorization from both owners; normal-workflow map and identified gap; target products and required deadlines; scope and input hashes; named approved roles; language and information-access parity; independent-review or declared non-independent-review arrangement; approved storage and retention; normal-process fallback; **numerical additional-person-minute ceiling and deadline safety buffer**; measurement start/end; and incident/escalation contacts retained privately.

The ceilings and buffers are **unset**, not zero. Owners must choose them from operating constraints before data collection; V10 invents neither an acceptable delay nor a staff-saving target. The validator returns `BLOCKED_FOR_LIVE_USE` while these entries are missing. A structurally complete form still requires authentic human authority; software cannot confer approval. A changed ceiling after results emerge is an amendment, not a retroactive pass.

## 4. Execution sequence

| Phase | Action | Exit record |
|---|---|---|
| A — Existing-workflow review | Walk one current source-to-clearance chain with the owners; identify which existing controls already handle the reference cases. | Retain existing process / close redundant proposal / specify gap / decline. |
| B — Public-source tabletop | Apply the fixed 61 obligations and [scenario cards](../data/census_v10/scenario_cards.csv) to support sheets. Preserve source qualifications; do not simulate institutional approval. | Completed review rubric, discrepancies and revised design proposal. This may precede live permission. |
| C — Local freeze | Complete the activation record and first-wave manifest. Freeze allocation, source conditions, deadlines, instruments and assessor rubric. | Signed, time-stamped case-local receipt; gate opens only for that case and window. |
| D — Non-displacing shadow | Normal production continues. A separate shadow preparer produces source-qualified support sheets only, using the agreed input snapshot. | Normal and shadow output hashes, workload and update logs; no shadow draft enters clearance automatically. |
| E — Paired review and closeout | Review both outputs against the same obligation set; adjudicate disagreements; reconcile all time and failures. | Case-specific feasibility readout and owner disposition. |
| F — Separate future decision | Only with new authorization, design a test replacing one named step and observing actual displaced work. | Not part of V10 execution; no inference of savings from phase D. |

Normal production must never depend on shadow completion. If a shadow reviewer discovers a potentially material error in a normal product, use the existing authorized correction route promptly; do not withhold a correction for experimental separation. Log the cross-method intervention as contamination, preserve the event and withhold an unaffected causal contrast.

**Learning and interference:** do not ask the same preparer to produce the same packet twice and call the time difference an efficiency effect. Prefer separate suitably qualified preparers with recorded prior exposure and comparable instructions. Use masked method labels for quality review when feasible; report when layout or content reveals the method. A method may be recognizable. Randomization is not implemented or claimed by this protocol. A later causal benchmark needs its own allocation, cluster/interference, power and carryover design before outcomes are seen.

Existing internal record systems and prior jointly prepared materials remain available to the normal method. They cannot be removed to manufacture a weaker comparator. The shadow method's setup, training and access advantages must be recorded. No new AI service, tool permission or external transfer is authorized; any separately approved tooling must be versioned and accounted for.

## 5. Comparable workload accounting

### Boundary and unit

The measurement unit is a **matched case × product bundle × input wave**, not a paragraph or a citation. Bundles may share source work: record the shared work once and retain its linkage, rather than duplicating minutes on every product. Keep both cases' results separate. Report the number of independent bundles and all dependencies; neither 64 observations nor 61 obligations is an independent-workflow sample size.

Log one exclusive work segment per pseudonymous actor. Record UTC start/end, active person-minutes, office/role, bundle, method, stage, capture basis and completion status. Stop the timer for interruptions and passive waiting; do not overlap an actor's timed segments. Four people in a 20-minute meeting contribute four 20-minute segments (**80 person-minutes**, one meeting), not 20 and not 320. General tasks that cannot be defensibly split remain at bundle level. An optional component allocation must sum to one for each segment and is never added back to the bundle total.

Stages include source retrieval, identity/version checking, reconciliation, fact-sheet preparation, substantive adaptation, review/clearance support, coordination, correction/rework and upkeep. Include aborted work and work shifted to another office or shared service. Do not repeat field missions, substantive negotiations, patrols or protected operational activity for this experiment.

### Five mutually exclusive accounting buckets

For bundle *b*, let **B** = normal-specific minutes; **S** = shadow-specific minutes; **C** = common production inputs performed once and used by both; **U** = one-time setup/training; **E** = evaluation-only time. Source commonality alone does not prove a task was performed once: the custodian must establish the shared-work boundary.

- Normal process description: `B + C`.
- Shadow process description: `S + C`.
- Actual trial burden: `B + S + C + U + E`, counting each recorded segment once.
- Descriptive matched contrast: `(B + C) − (S + C)` **only when task scope, sources, language, review requirements and time capture are comparable**.

The contrast is **not observed time saved**: normal work still occurred and was not displaced. Report U and E separately but do not hide them when discussing implementation burden. Shared input costs appear in both alternative process descriptions; they are counted once in actual trial expenditure. No separately additive benefits are attached to C09, C02 and C05 or to overlapping V8/V9 infrastructure.

### Missingness and comparability

Use the [batch register](../data/census_v10/templates/batches.csv) to declare expected tasks, completeness and parity before calculating contrasts. Missing time is unknown, not zero. Preserve an explicit missing segment and reconcile the ledger to the expected task roster and participant roster in approved storage. Zero rows in a mode are not proof that it required zero work. The readout requires an explicit observed segment for each production mode; a zero-time record needs a custodian-documented no-work finding in the task roster, not an imputed zero.

Show recorded minutes, estimated minutes, missing-segment counts, unfinished tasks and the capture fraction. When capture is incomplete or a clock record is estimated, suppress a complete-process total and matched contrast; recorded partial time can be displayed only as incomplete coverage. A completion failure does not disappear from the table because no final output exists. Common labor and review quality must be comparable; faster unfinished or lower-quality production is not a favorable outcome.

The supplied dependency-free R readout computes **descriptive accounting only**. It accepts empty templates without producing a result estimate. Filled live logs must run in the locally approved environment, not in public CI or this repository. The synthetic fixtures verify arithmetic and refusal behavior; they are never operational observations.

## 6. Measures and decision interpretation

The definitions below operationalize the existing V8/V9 M01–M07 specifications; they do not replace earlier gates or introduce an unsupported performance target.

| Measure | Numerator / calculation | Denominator and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| M01 — Traceable fulfillment | Obligations fulfilled with a valid source locator, exact version and required attribution | All enrolled review obligations; show owner-only and party-attribution controls separately. Missing outputs stay unfulfilled. |
| M02 — Qualification fidelity | Obligations with any lost/altered required date, stage, role, count qualifier, unit, scope or attribution; also show failures by field | All enrolled obligations requiring those qualifications. Record first-pass and post-correction results, not only corrected success. |
| M03 — Update handling | Authorized release to receiver acknowledgement, then to product-specific accepted update | All eligible update waves. Report pending/unacknowledged updates and elapsed time at closeout separately; do not zero-fill or treat them as completed latencies. |
| M04 — Workload | B, S, C and the descriptive contrast when eligible | Identical matched bundles; report all-enrolled completion and capture before complete-pair summaries. Never label the contrast staff hours saved. |
| M05 — Added burden | U, E, upkeep, additional review and transferred work | Whole case window and each office's contribution; retain failed/aborted work. |
| M06 — Protected operation | Deadline risks/breaches, approval changes, unauthorized disclosures and material factual failures | All products, transfers and incidents, including stopped cases; no offset against a time advantage. |
| M07 — Finance | No numerical result in V10 | Requires later actual substitution, eligible cancelable cost objects and added/transition costs. Capacity is not cash. |

Every rubric row records expected action, source/version, missingness, first-pass judgment, materiality, correction, final judgment and assessor. A faithfully reported disagreement can pass; falsely reconciling it cannot. A party statement passes only with its party attribution preserved. Keeping an owner-only analysis out of the common drafting layer is successful boundary preservation, not a failed opportunity.

For consequential disagreements, use an agreed adjudicator outside the preparation role. Retain both initial assessments and rationale; do not resolve source truth by automatic majority or by preferring the later report without evidence. Primary review uses originals and qualifications, not the other generated output as ground truth. Separate identifiable source facts, source interpretations, research inferences and recommendations in every sheet.

**Analysis plan:** descriptive case/bundle results with exact numerators, denominators, missingness and first/final status. No pooled efficacy claim, p-value, non-inferiority declaration, precision claim from correlated observations, annualized time saving or monetization. Report case heterogeneity and unfavorable outcomes. A later inferential design needs a prespecified independent unit, allocation and uncertainty method; the present two selected contexts do not provide those.

## 7. Source and attribution safeguards

A reusable assertion travels with its namespace, source symbol/URL, source-file hash, page/paragraph, publisher or speaker, event date/precision, issue date, actual access/availability basis, observation cutoff, units, inequalities, implementation stage, revision and intended product owner. Links are not permissions. A party's annex remains a party assertion even inside an official UN report.

Keep primary observations distinct from references, corroboration and proposals. Reciprocal referrals do not independently verify a fact. Do not count a launch and its publication as two workshops, an existing crossing enlargement as a new crossing, committee membership as attendance, or mixed-stage projects as completed work. Store competing values with an unresolved-conflict flag; no ratio is authorized when its denominator is unresolved. Support sheets must preserve missing values as missing.

For the public tabletop, historical publication/availability proxies remain explicitly uncertain. It tests representational fidelity, not what an official could have known in real time. For the authorized shadow, use actual approved input-access timestamps; no later evidence silently enters an earlier output snapshot. Corrections produce a new version and a link to the superseded assertion.

## 8. Stops, fallback and restart

| Trigger | Immediate response | Scope and restart condition |
|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized disclosure/transfer, restricted input, or loss of access control | Stop affected processing; restrict access through existing incident procedures; notify designated authority. | Case-wide hold where exposure may cross bundles. Restart only after authorized remediation and written clearance. |
| Attempt to replace a mandated output, transfer approval authority or introduce unapproved tooling | Stop the proposed action; continue the established authorized process. | No restart on software approval alone; separate institutional decision required. |
| Required-product deadline at risk, or case safety buffer reached | Stop shadow dependence/work on that product and revert to normal production. | Normal delivery takes priority. Review the incident before a new window. |
| Material source misattribution, invented fact, concealed conflict or false implementation stage | Quarantine affected shadow sheet; prevent its use; invoke existing correction route if normal production is affected. | A material released error triggers case review. Retain failure and rework time; re-review affected outputs before restart. |
| Missing source/version evidence or failed hash check | Suspend affected assertion's scoring or reuse; retain its enrolled disposition. | Recover/authenticate or use qualified withholding through the normal owner; never invent absence. |
| Unequal input snapshot, task/language/review conditions, carryover or cross-method contamination | Stop the quantitative comparison, not necessary normal work. | Mark non-comparable; a new matched wave needs a pre-outcome receipt. |
| Additional-effort ceiling exceeded, unlogged transferred work or material time-capture failure | Stop incremental pilot work or its workload comparison; reconcile burden. | No retroactive ceiling increase. Retain all observed and unknown effort. |
| Owners find equivalent current controls, no specific gap, or withdraw permission | Close/stop the added pilot for that case. | Retain current workflow; do not continue to obtain a favorable result. |

Any participant can raise a stop. The designated owner controls resumption in their remit; unresolved ownership conflicts keep the affected case paused. Record trigger time, affected hashes/IDs, normal-process fallback, responsible role, exposure/correction action and resumption decision in the incident register. Preserve the pre-stop data under approved retention, not as a public attachment.

No optional stopping on apparent time gains. At the predeclared end, unresolved material failures, deadline risks or authority/security issues yield **do not advance**. Incomplete or non-comparable measurement yields **inconclusive**, not success. A design that adds no value beyond the existing workflow yields **retain existing process**. A favorable, properly bounded result yields **consider a separately authorized substitution test**, not implementation or savings approval.

## 9. Information handling and publication boundary

Use minimum necessary pseudonymous workload information for process evaluation only, not staff ranking or performance decisions. Keep the re-identification key local and separate; role/time combinations can remain identifying. Owners set the lawful/authorized collection basis, access list, retention duration and destruction or archival route before collection. Avoid sensitive free text in logs; use controlled reason codes and a separately protected incident reference.

The proposed safeguards are consistent with the UN system's principles of purpose specification, necessity, limited retention, confidentiality, security and accountability [R3]. The principles do not themselves approve a storage service, staff study or external transfer. Local applicable procedures must be confirmed by the relevant authority.

Only public source manifests, design templates, deliberately synthetic tests and disclosure-reviewed aggregate findings belong in this public repository. **Never commit live logs, staff identities, internal drafts, private custodian/contact details or incident narratives.** Hashes of sensitive material also require a disclosure decision; hashing is not automatic anonymization. The CI workflow is limited to public design and synthetic fixtures. It has no schedule and performs no course monitoring.

## 10. Owner readout and next decision

The closeout must show: gap assessed and baseline arrangement; permission and exact case/window; all-enrolled scope and amendments; matched versus non-comparable bundles; first-pass/final quality; pending updates; B/S/C/U/E accounting and capture coverage; incidents and added/shifted work; limitations; and the owners' decision. Record dissent and rejected opportunities.

The preparation can be complete while live-use gates remain closed. Owners may approve one case and decline the other. C02/C05 remain the same control workstream rather than independently additive savings; C09 retains its V8 role; all parent and mission ranks and the 20/32 earlier retrospective support remain unchanged. A later financial assessment requires substantiated replacement and cancelable cost objects—not citation counts, gross mission budgets or salary multiplication.

**Next executable research step:** run the public-source tabletop with the fixed obligations and independent rubric review. The subsequent live shadow requires the case-local approvals and complete activation record. No automatic start follows from a passed software test or delivery of this package.
