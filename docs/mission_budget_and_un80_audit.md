# Mission-budget extension and UN80 implementation / cost-allocation audit

**As of 8 October 2026. Phase: documentary and financial-boundary audit; no independently verified overlap or net incremental savings.**

## Bottom line

Six separately budgeted field operations now have source-linked, category-level financial registers, along with 18 linked function comparisons and a five-unit DPO support-account allocation bridge. This substantially improves the baseline's coverage of military/police costs, civilians, operational expenditure, transitions, and pre-budgeted restructuring. It does **not** establish redundant posts or spending.

**Most consequential finding:** The DPO 'other assessed' 2027 estimate of **$95.4641 million** in [Section 5](../data/mission_source_extension.csv) is the **same DPO support-account allocation** presented in [A/80/631, p.44](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4109170/files/A_80_631-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=44). Adding these figures would double-count the same source of financing. Likewise, the proposed **$27.9426 million** for 'Office for Mine Action and Police, Peacebuilding and Peace Support Office' is temporarily **combined**, and its footnote states the structure is subject to General Assembly approval, including four post redeployments. It is **not** a standalone cost of the new peacebuilding office.

### Priority field cases

- **MINUSCA — PBF and mission-delivered activities.** [A/80/623 pp.4 and 11](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4105784/files/A_80_623-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=11) includes an electoral-phase restructuring and describes DDR, justice, and local engagement. [Section 3 Annex III pp.128–129](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4110936/files/A_81_6_%28Sect._3%29-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=128) proposes CAR PBF programmes involving justice, governance, and social cohesion. The programmes share a country and policy domain, not necessarily duplicate payees, grants or deliverables. Validate project IDs, field delivery contracts, recipient lists and locations. The **151 proposed abolished posts/positions** are *already in* the mission's 2026/27 financial baseline.
- **MONUSCO — mandate-adjusted rather than mechanical cuts.** [A/80/624 p.17, paras. 56–59](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4105808/files/A_80_624-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=17) describes planned procurement/service consolidation, **150 abolishments** and **42 nationalizations**, while also recording expanded ceasefire-support requirements from Security Council resolution 2808 (2025). It is incorrect to classify the budget change as the result of a completed UN80 measure or to count those baseline reductions as new savings.
- **UNIFIL — mandatory transition period.** [A/80/634 pp.3–4](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4107117/files/A_80_634-EN.pdf#page=4) budgets **six months of operations** and **six months of drawdown**, with a 31 December 2026 cessation of operations. [Section 5 §5.II.20 p.45](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%205%29#page=45) explains that UNTSO continues a distinct mandate. Review SLAs and transferability of logistics, vehicles, premises and contracts; the **$144.8m proposal decrease is not a recurring efficiency forecast**.
- **UNISFA / UNMISS — Abyei programming and logistics.** [A/80/614 pp.3–4](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4106060/files/A_80_614-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=3) and [Section 5 p.9](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%205%29#page=9) identify peacebuilding and UN-country-team involvement; UNISFA reports enhanced logistical cooperation with UNMISS. The right test is whether a given funded output, service or contract is charged twice, not whether the two missions both mention Abyei.
- **UNSOS / UNOAU — field logistics versus political and operational partnership.** [A/80/628 summary](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4105782/files/A_80_628-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=3) describes AUSSOM logistical support, UNTMIS handover and **nine planned HRDD-related posts**. [UNOAU §3.IV.4 pp.84–85](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4110936/files/A_81_6_%28Sect._3%29-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=84) concerns strategic AU engagement and capacity support. The services are complementary until finance and service request records establish otherwise.

## UN80 implementation audit

The updated [UN80 audit register](../data/un80_implementation_audit.csv) retains all **11 U01–U11** reform identifiers and distinguishes:
- explicitly described shared structures (regional political-operational, Peacebuilding and Peace Support Office, joint Gender Unit);
- proposed closer coordination (policy, training, innovation, partnerships, information integrity);
- documented but different arrangements (joint disability action plan; previously existing Coordination and Shared Services Office);
- **UNOAU's proposed internal merger, explicitly not labelled a substantive UN80 adjustment**.

**No reform is certified operationally implemented.** The FY2026/27 [support-account report p.44](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4109170/files/A_80_631-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=44) is evidence of *proposed financial allocations and staffing presentations*, not transaction-level evidence of realized merger savings.

The support account's headline proposal is **$383.4m** (core **$320.0m**, separate additional corporate/initiative provisions **$63.4m**) compared with **$425.6m** previously approved. This comparison is a *proposed budget difference*, not money already saved. The source also reports **1,357** proposed posts and general temporary assistance positions versus **1,529** originally approved for 2025/26 and **1,327** in *revised* 2025/26 estimates. Therefore an '172 positions cut' narrative would ignore the more relevant revised baseline. [A/80/631 p.3](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4109170/files/A_80_631-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=3)

## Cost-allocation audit: rules

1. Always associate dollar amounts with **funding stream, entity, budget object, period, status and inclusion hierarchy**. Unique projects or salaries require matching cost codes or post IDs.
2. DPO 2027 regular budget **$7.3512m**, other assessed **$95.4641m**, and extrabudgetary **$88.6947m** are components of its **$191.5100m** departmental total, not three additional budgets. Source: [Section 5 table 5.I.18 pp.29–30](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%205%29#page=30).
3. The [Section 3 DPPA Peacebuilding and Peace Support Office](https://digitallibrary.un.org/nanna/record/4110936/files/A_81_6_%28Sect._3%29-EN.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0&withWatermark=0#page=59) proposed regular-budget total **$54.1066m** contains **$50.0m in grants**. That cannot be attributed to departmental staff costs.
4. UNTSO (**$40.7002m**) and UNMOGIP (**$8.8804m**) are separate regular-budget entities presented in Section 5, not included in the **DPO-only $191.51m** subtotal.
5. The **36 special political missions** remain provisionally included as an **aggregate** in Section 3; their detailed **2027 addenda** and unique mission cost centres have not been comprehensively reconciled. The source discrepancy between **$37.7001m** and **$32.7405m** extrabudgetary estimates remains unresolved.
6. A proposal budget difference must **never** be relabelled operational savings without invoice/post-ledger evidence, preserved service outputs and a feasible counterfactual.

## Outstanding research and validation gates

1. Obtain the detailed SPM cluster addenda A/81/6 (Sect.3)/Add.1–4 (those confirmed publicly at the time of review include references to Add.1–3) and reconcile mission post/cost objects with peacekeeping accounts.
2. Compare the six SG mission **proposals** against the **final GA resolutions** [80/275, 80/276, 80/278, 80/282, 80/283 and 80/285](https://www.un.org/en/ga/fifth/80/resdec80.shtml) before describing appropriated amounts.
3. Acquire 2026/27 actual cost ledgers, consultant/vendor accounts, chargebacks, field SLAs, unique post IDs and 2025/26 *revised* support-account allocations.
4. Collect dated GA approvals and implementation orders for the combined Mine Action/Police–PBPS structure and staff redeployments.
5. Apply a counterfactual: recurring activity costs to remove **minus** replacement delivery, transition and compliance costs, with full safety/mandate gates.

**Release verdict:** mission budget coverage expanded; published arithmetic and identity of selected support-account allocations verified; **zero** specific excess posts, executed new cuts or net incremental dollars can yet be certified. “Not estimated” must not be interpreted as zero.
