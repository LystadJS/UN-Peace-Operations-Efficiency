# Peace operations efficiency portfolio: leadership assessment

**As of 23 October 2026, the project has a public validation baseline and fourteen screening cases; it does not yet have a decision-grade operational audit portfolio.** The intended outcome is specific, evidenced improvements to peacekeeping services that protect mandate delivery and meet applicable adopted reforms. There is no positive savings quota: retaining an effective arrangement or rejecting an unsupported intervention can be the correct decision. None of the fourteen cases has completed substantive operational adjudication, and financial benefits remain unknown. The [completion standard](desired_end_state.md) governs that distinction.

**The repository now supports controlled research, but its data coverage exceeds its validated operational knowledge.** The [dataset registry](dataset_registry.md) and [contracts](../data/portfolio/dataset_contracts.json) retain 324 historical data artifacts. Thirty-seven have repository semantic review; 287 have provisional structural profiles. A semantic review is not operational-owner acceptance, a verified join or a complete transaction population. The [coverage matrix](portfolio_coverage.md) separates entity identity, financial coverage, function mapping, reform applicability and operational evidence. Its eleven assessed-resource records and 36 SPM proposal records are distinct source populations, not a worldwide coverage denominator. The [94 canonical identities](canonical_entities.md) include missions, departments, support units, functional labels and allocation pools; they do not establish current lifecycle or mandate coverage.

The strongest retained quantitative inputs describe resources and proposals. The [financial baseline](../data/portfolio/financial_baseline.json), with an underlying 8 October cutoff, preserves these boundaries:

| Six missions, maintenance gross, July 2026–June 2027 | $4,028.1880 million | Subset of the eleven-entity maintenance register |
| Eleven assessed entities, maintenance gross, same fiscal period | $4,625.9401 million | Retained approved-resource-note scope |
| Six missions, inclusive appropriation | $4,442.1194 million | Already includes Support Account, UNLB and RSCE shares |

These are overlapping perimeters, periods and financial statuses; they must not be added. The SPM corpus also identifies $14.6925 million of already embedded proposed efficiencies. Those measures belong in the baseline, rather than becoming newly attributable project benefits. A reduced appropriation, internal charge or post establishment does not establish a lower whole-UN cost or a realized saving.

**The reform baseline establishes selected authority, not complete compliance.** The [GA80 review](unga80_reform_baseline.md) freshly retrieved two final adopted texts and extracted 65 selected provision records. They include institutional directions, requests and safeguards, not 65 universally applicable mission mandates. Mission-financing final texts, other relevant instruments, amendments and paragraph-specific incorporation of advice still require review. The [GA81 register](unga81_reform_baseline.md) is a bounded ongoing-session review: no relevant adopted reform obligation was authenticated and extracted. That evidence limitation does not mean no obligation exists. A/81/6 proposals and A/81/7 advice remain distinct from adopted action.

The [eleven-arrangement reconciliation](../data/reforms/un80_arrangement_reconciliation.json) differentiates proposals, coordination and reported implementation. In particular, U02 has management-reported implementation evidence; it lacks the operational records needed to verify the full arrangement, cost effects or benefit. The [compliance view](reform_compliance_view.md) reports missing evidence and future deadlines without treating silence as noncompliance. The [separate-agent review](reform_independent_review.md) records its limited primary-source inspection and GA81 retrieval constraints. It is an agent review, not a UN audit, legal opinion or institutional assurance.

**The cases identify plausible mechanisms to test, not demonstrated waste.** [C01–C03](cases/C01.md) prioritize RSCE charging and retained local work, UNIFIL–UNSCOL asset transfers, and BINUH–UNSOH support. [C04–C08](cases/C04.md) examine UNISFA translation and transferred assets, UNSOS contractor recovery, MONUSCO medical-insurance controls, and continuity after UNMISS post changes. [C09–C14](cases/C09.md) examine course-level administration, regional workflows, UNOWAS–UNOCA and Cyprus factual support, UNIFIL residual liabilities and existing joint structures. Individual dossiers specify evidence, alternatives, safeguards and proposed custodian roles. All fourteen remain `SCREENING_CANDIDATE` with `BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE`; accountable owners are unconfirmed.

Several useful inferences are already warranted. Joint structures and related functions justify checking residual task ownership, but cannot establish duplication. UNMISS's 53 Electoral Affairs abolishments are part of the retained total of 91, already an adopted baseline action. UNIFIL's drawdown requires residual-liability and service-continuity analysis; its six-month operational budget cannot be annualized as recurring benefit. Public training totals require course-run identity and outcome evidence before consolidation. Reporting cases must compare against the current jointly supported workflow so that a proposed new control does not duplicate a functioning one. The [owner-review package](consolidated_owner_review_decision_memo.md) still has no approvals or live trials; both reporting live-use gates remain closed and the course watch remains paused.

**Consolidation must protect both analytical integrity and operational capability.** Repository controls have unified issue migration, current-status generation, artifact designation and validation planning while preserving historical research. The [lineage audit](repository_lineage.md) still records 196 unresolved dynamic dependencies in its original frozen scope. These require resolution or explicit limitations before relying on affected outputs. The [CI plan](ci_consolidation_plan.md) and [publication controls](publication_workflow.md) separate routine read-only validation from explicit candidate publication. Passing those checks establishes software properties, not operational efficiency.

Potential operational consolidation is grouped under [twelve nonadditivity pools](../data/portfolio/benefit_pools.json). Shared HQ support, regional workflows, training, report production and Lebanon transition span multiple cases. These groups flag potential common cost objects, not proven identical expenditure. Parent investigations carry no benefit total; mutually exclusive alternatives and repeated cost objects must be reconciled once. Provider external cost, transferred work, retained staff and transition liabilities remain inside the system perimeter. The [verified-benefit ledger](../data/portfolio/verified_benefit_ledger.json) has no recognized entries and null totals. Unknown is different from measured zero.

**The remaining constraint is admissible evidence and accountable review.** Draft requests and [pilot charters](../data/pilots/charters.json) specify linked transactions, assets, post actions, workloads and outcomes for the three initial audits. Custodian roles must be confirmed; preparing a request does not send it or supply consent. Approved restricted intake must precede sensitive records. Case-specific observation periods and numeric stop thresholds require actual baselines and owner acceptance. [Synthetic rehearsals](../data/pilots/rehearsal_receipt.json) exercise method behavior; they cannot substitute for actual records. All [21 operational audit steps](operational_adjudication_blockers.md) remain blocked and unexecuted. Implementation and realization are [conditional future work](realization_workflow.md), with no approved intervention or live execution.

| Completion gate | Actual position |
| --- | --- |
| 1 — Foundation | Partial: controls and identities exist; lifecycle, mandate, full instrument and operational-perimeter coverage remain incomplete. |
| 2 — Validation system | Partial: dossiers, contracts and rehearsals exist; authorized records, accepted baselines and prospective thresholds remain missing. |
| 3 — Decision portfolio | Blocked: zero substantive operational audits against the minimum three across two service types; no financial adjudication. |
| 4 — Realization or accepted no-change decision | Blocked: no implementation evidence, verified realization or substantively adjudicated accountable no-change acceptance. |

The next leadership decision is to nominate competent owners and authorize the minimum evidence path for RSCE, UNIFIL–UNSCOL and BINUH–UNSOH, while retaining the other cases and their gaps. Review the actual baseline and feasible alternative, independently adjudicate the full cost and service consequences, and only then consider implementation. No monetary finding may be called savings before `FINANCIALLY_VALIDATED`. This release makes that work reviewable; it does not declare it complete.
