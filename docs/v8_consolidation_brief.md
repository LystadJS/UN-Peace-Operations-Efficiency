# Consolidation decision brief — V8

**15 October 2026 · UN Peace Operations Efficiency · Mandates and financial controls unchanged**

## Recommended decision

**Take one UNOWAS–UNOCA shared-evidence pilot to the existing workflow owners for comparison and a controlled shadow demonstration. Do not merge reporting, create a new platform, reduce posts or book savings.** The prototype makes reuse inspectable; it does not establish that existing internal preparation is duplicated.

The implementable unit is a source-attributed component register with product-specific snapshots: common factual inputs where warranted, separate political assessments and approvals, and dated updates for each briefing. Its benefit must be incremental to the joint work and cross-references already observed. [Full assessment](v8_shared_production_assessment.md).

## Evidence that changes implementation readiness

The worked cycle covers **S/2025/187, S/PV.9890, S/2025/342 and S/PV.9931**, supported by two official joint-activity notices. The three core activities have **nine selected appearances** across the two reports and the April UNOWAS briefing. UNOCA’s June briefing instead provides an explicit attributed reference to the earlier UNOWAS statement. A common register can represent these relationships without force-fitting every topic into every output. [Worked cycle](v8_worked_reporting_cycle.md), [component matrix](../data/census_v8/source_component_matrix.csv).

The pilot’s most concrete contribution is **controlled handling of differences**, not removal of products. It preserves 27/28 January source disagreement, distinguishes Forum duration from representative attendance, retains “more than 30” and “workplan adopted,” separates a publication from its launch workshop, and does not turn an announced withdrawal into completed withdrawal. Later source information is withheld from earlier replay views. Report availability proxies are explicitly uncertain, so this remains a retrospective reconstruction. [Claim ledger](../data/census_v8/claim_ledger.csv), [source metadata](../data/census_v8/pilot_design.json).

## Proposed implementation package

Use one existing repository or approved records system for canonical component IDs, source hashes/locators, event and publication dates, factual qualifications, and reviewer status. Give each office a separately frozen subset and retain its existing report and briefing approvals. Do not require synchronized deadlines. Unresolved dates should trigger qualified wording or owner adjudication, not a majority-vote “correction.”

The prototype supplies the data model, four worked support sheets, reversible source observations, a measurement specification and corruption tests. It assigns no staff and accesses no internal drafts or confidential contacts. **57 representation/source checks passed**, including **18 deliberately corrupted fixtures rejected**. Those results establish defined software behavior on this corpus, not human clearance, operational performance or savings. [Acceptance receipt](../data/census_v8/acceptance_receipt.json).

## Gate before any operational or financial claim

| Gate | What the offices would need to establish | Current result |
|---|---|---|
| **Existing-workflow comparison** | Whether this function already exists, and the precise repeated preparation step to replace. | Unverified; existing joint delivery and referrals are already baseline. |
| **Ownership and mandate protection** | Accepted factual custodian, review responsibilities, separate approvals and deadline fallback. | Proposed only; no institutional authorization obtained. |
| **Shadow-cycle quality** | Source fidelity, conflicts, update latency and maintenance burden on the same selected claim population. | Technical retrospective demo complete; live shadow trial not run. |
| **Actual substitution** | Evidence that a named step can disappear without transferring hidden work or reducing required quality. | Not tested. |
| **Cost and cash release** | Eligible cancelable cost objects, added operating costs, transition liabilities and funding boundaries. | Not established; no monetary estimate. |

A shadow workflow run in addition to the normal process cannot by itself establish avoided labor. Before/after staff time would also need comparable scope and workload; it is not automatically a causal estimate. Keep any capacity gain distinct from recoverable cash. [Measurement plan](../data/census_v8/measurement_plan.csv), [full financial boundary](v8_shared_production_assessment.md#6-financial-boundary-and-conditional-decision).

## Alternatives and stop conditions

**Retain the current process** if the offices already maintain equivalent source/version controls. **Test the lightweight overlay** where a specific gap is demonstrated. **Do not advance a centralized drafting unit or office merger** on this evidence: distinct analysis, approvals and mandated outputs remain necessary boundaries of the pilot.

Stop or revert if the overlay delays a required product, obscures source disagreement, transfers restricted information, or costs more to maintain than the verified work it replaces. No budget or post action should proceed without separate substantiation and the applicable authorization.

**Disposition:** a documented design ready for owner review, not an approved consolidation. No duplicate paid service or incremental net saving is established. V8 preserves pre-V8 evidence and rankings. The course watch remains paused; no scheduled or manual course-completion check was run for this assessment.
