# Public-source efficiency priorities — Executive brief

**8 October 2026 | UN Peace Operations Efficiency**

**Decision point:** Authorize further public-document and workflow validation, not additional mandated cuts. The register contains **14 immediately researchable cases**, **three already-budgeted measures to monitor**, and **one protected legal negative control**. No additional net savings are independently established.

| Investigation | Decision-ready public action | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| UNIFIL → UNSCOL | Check six-vehicle, ICT and ration asset-transfer plan against GA withdrawal requirements | $438,100 already priced in the **2027 proposal** |
| BINUH ↔ UNSOH | Separate shared aviation from wider reimbursed support activities | $1.4m already priced, $4m a proposed broader transfer charge |
| RSCE ↔ SPMs | Check Kuwait client transfer and proposed staffing/finance structure without double charges | $3.2591m proposed SPM funding share, **not savings** |
| UNISFA | Follow GA-directed translation and MINUSMA-transferred/underused asset reviews | No independently costed eligible savings |
| UNSOS, MONUSCO, UNMISS | Audit required contractor recovery, medical claim controls and execution of approved civilian post changes | Neither recovered sums nor new savings established |
| UNOWAS/UNOCA, UNAMA/UNRCCA, DPPA/DPO desks and training | Compare publicly reported products, audiences, and procurement workflows | Shared subject matter is not proof of duplication |

**Financial guardrails:** The $14.6925m of 2027 SPM estimated efficiencies is already part of the SG proposal. The 2027 SPM $422.671m remains proposed, whereas six selected peacekeeping missions' $4.028188bn maintenance funding covers **GA-approved July 2026–June 2027** spending. Never aggregate those as a single addressable cost pool.

**Evidence update:** Official ACABQ listings identify A/81/7 (6 Oct 2026), A/81/7/Add.2 and Add.3 (25 Aug 2026). Their recommendation details have not yet been evaluated, and they are not approved appropriations.
