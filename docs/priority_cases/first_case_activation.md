## Separate research maturity from access

F03 `VALIDATION_READY` requires an exact intervention, **proposed** owner role/custodian, mandate and cost perimeter, evidence request, counterfactual design, prospective guardrails and nonadditivity group. It does not require accepted institutional ownership, actual observed outcomes or a sent request. Accepted accountable ownership is expressly required at `IMPLEMENTATION_READY`; collection and live intervention have their own authority gates.

The following checklist applies to each of C01, C02 and C03. It is a research-design review, **not a transition decision**. No mandatory check is declared literally true and no candidate state changes.

| F03 check | Existing evidence | Work needed before a reviewed readiness decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bounded intervention | Case mechanism, pilot and proposed minimal slice | Freeze the exact client/service or asset-requirement cohort, affected outputs, dates and exclusions before reviewing outcomes. |
| Owner role and custodian | Named roles in E01–E03 and pilot charters | Proposed-role design is present. Confirm actual custody and authority separately before dispatch or collection. |
| Mandate and cost perimeter | Case-specific financial boundaries and cautions | Attach action-specific, verified mandate/competence receipts to the exact unit. A general mandate or proposed budget alone does not authorize the change. |
| Evidence request | Existing purpose-bound field contracts | Design is present; sending, authorization and receipt remain separate unavailable facts. |
| Counterfactual design | Same-scope comparisons in each charter | Design is present; freeze the precise frame and retain adverse/null/no-change alternatives. Actual comparability is a later evidence test. |
| Prospective guardrails | Metrics, dimensions and stop/fallback principles | Substantively review sufficiency for the chosen research unit; specify baseline-measurement/threshold-setting before outcome review. Numeric thresholds remain unset. Unaccepted ownership alone is not a F03 failure. |
| Nonadditivity group | Registered CASE/C mapping and benefit-pool memberships | Preserve memberships and allocation limits; no monetary benefit is recognized. |

This distinction lets public-source and design work progress while internal access remains blocked. It does not convert a complete design into `OPERATIONALLY_VALIDATED` evidence.

## C01 — RSCE: one client, one service, one closed period

Propose one jointly selected client and service category with an entire closed accounting-period population. Obtain client debit and provider credit lines, linked reversals/settlements, the approved allocation denominator and independent control totals. Include unmatched and corrected items. Pair the charge population with the corresponding retained local work, completed units, hours, external cost and service-level/exception observations.

The denominator may cover a wider allocation base; request an approved non-identifying denominator/control-total receipt instead of unrelated clients' identifiable transactions. The first decision is whether the charge and actual work can be explained or whether a bounded discrepancy warrants fuller validation. Required local decisions, approvals and segregation-of-duties controls are legitimate explanations to test.

Public inputs establish reported service responsibilities, client scope, finance perimeters and authority context. Restricted fields remain exactly those in [E01](../../data/evidence_requests/E01_rsce.json). Custodian roles are the RSCE controller or authorized finance custodian and the client mission's finance/service-line managers; no role is accepted.

**Minimum receipt:** complete extraction definitions, counts and gross signed control totals; allocation approval; correction/settlement lag; retained-task explanation; SLA definition and failure/missingness coverage. One client-period cannot establish RSCE-wide overbilling, staffing efficiency or post savings.

## C02 — UNIFIL–UNSCOL: one pre-existing requirement and asset chain

Propose one agreed asset class and one pre-existing UNSCOL requirement or complete small requirement cohort, fixed before outcomes are examined. Include proposed assets never transferred, rejected or unavailable as well as completed transfers. Trace requirement chronology, custody, serviceable condition, recipient acceptance and deployment to the credible procurement alternative.

Request actual transport/preparation, retained maintenance/storage, disposal and residual costs. Establish whether an otherwise-needed purchase was feasible and cancellable; a hypothetical list price is insufficient. Preserve source-mission readiness and transition obligations. Public transition and asset-reuse statements do not supply a signed handover or canceled purchase.

Use [E02](../../data/evidence_requests/E02_assets.json) within approved restricted storage. Proposed custodians are UNIFIL property/finance/transition and UNSCOL property/procurement/finance/receiving-service roles. **Minimum receipt:** eligible requirement/asset population and exclusions; need/procurement chronology; custody/condition/acceptance; full cost coverage; continuing readiness obligations. One chain cannot establish portfolio-wide avoidance or recurring savings.

## C03 — BINUH–UNSOH: one service and closed billing period

Propose one jointly selected support service and a complete closed billing-period population. Link BINUH and UNSOH postings to supplier cost and delivered units, retaining corrections, late settlements and unmatched items. Select comparable periods only after the service/handover chronology, accrual basis and settlement lag are understood.

Identify whether aviation is contained within a broader support package; internal reimbursement is not external whole-system expense. Include retained local work and transition cost. Use security-safe availability/quality indicators; precise flight routes, operating schedules and narrative security information are unnecessary public outputs.

Use [E03](../../data/evidence_requests/E03_haiti.json); proposed custodians are both entities' support and finance accountable roles. **Minimum receipt:** both extraction definitions and control totals; supplier expense and scope; complete delivered-unit/availability evidence; aviation/support inclusion bridge. One billing period cannot establish aviation-wide utilization or recurring benefit.

## Access decision and secure receipt

For each case, an institutionally competent owner/custodian must confirm purpose, lawful authority, classification, permitted fields, access, approved storage, retention and analyst roles. Dates, sampling frame and burden are **unset**. Do not promise a zero-burden export or invent a staff-hour estimate. Any review should begin with the smallest complete slice that resolves a defined uncertainty, rather than whichever large budget looks attractive.

A restricted evidence receipt should identify the accepted owner/custodian and competent-access approval; contract/version; eligible population and exclusions; entity/service/period; source system/extraction date; counts/control totals; correction lag; missing fields; source hashes and controlled locator; permitted access/retention/disclosure; and accepted burden. Raw records, pseudonymous tokens, asset mappings, re-identification tables and private storage locations stay outside public Git. Only separately disclosure-reviewed aggregates and opaque receipts may become public derivatives.

Choose the first operational-access case only when actual record availability, appropriate authority, accepted burden and a complete decision-relevant linkage are confirmed. At present there is no defensible access ranking. A failed, adverse or null result must not be a reason to substitute a more favorable case.
