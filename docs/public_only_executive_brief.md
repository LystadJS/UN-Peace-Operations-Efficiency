# UN Peace Operations Efficiency — Executive decision brief

<small>8 October 2026 · Public-source audit · SG 2027 proposals, GA 2026 approved mission budgets, and issued ACABQ A/81/7/Add.2–3</small>

## Decision in one paragraph

**Advance shared-service, cross-mission transition and joint regional-workflow validation; do not order mandate reductions or book additional savings yet.** The 2027 mission proposals are fully reconciled across 36 SPMs, including the previously unreviewed nine Cluster III missions. The ACABQ's issued reports provide budget advice for 27 missions (Clusters I and II) and general cross-cutting recommendations in A/81/7. The user reports that A/81/7/Add.1 and Add.4 have not yet been issued, so the nine Cluster III **ACABQ-specific** recommendations and the full ACABQ-adjusted SPM total remain **not determined**. Existing mission proposals already contain considerable efficiencies; the remaining question is whether separable *additional* costs can be avoided while protecting mandated outcomes.

## Cluster III implementation checkpoints

**BINUH:** Verify UNSOH service handover and aviation cost recovery; **$1.4M already booked**, broader service reimbursement is not comparable to a single aviation change. **UNSCOL:** Trace UNIFIL vehicles/IT/rations; **$438.1k is part of $446.8k in UNSCOL's already priced initiative total**. **UNAMA:** Protect Afghanistan human-rights and political reporting while checking proposed staffing/operational reductions. **UNSMIL and Colombia:** Validate facility and flight changes against deployment, security and verification obligations. **UNOWAS, UNOCA, UNRCCA and the Cameroon–Nigeria Commission:** Compare unique reports, missions, convenings and shared administrative facilities without assuming two regional mandates are interchangeable.

## Decision and remaining controls

**Authorize:** Public, output-level and service-design validation plus subsequent implementation monitoring. **Do not authorize on the present evidence:** mission merger, further staff abolishments, recognition of contract-level duplicate expenditure, or numerical net savings estimates. Actual invoices, asset handovers and unique payroll/cost-centre records remain unavailable until the controlled transaction audit.

The **2027 $422.4885M partial-advice carry-forward scenario** in the research package mechanically applies issued Cluster I/II advice while keeping unissued Cluster III/RSCE recommendations at the SG proposal. It is **not an official ACABQ recommendation or an appropriation** and should not be used as the endorsed budget in decision memoranda.
