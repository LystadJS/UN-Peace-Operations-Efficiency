# UN Peace Operations Efficiency

**Research baseline:** 2027 proposed programme budgets · **Status:** exploratory, source-linked, no validated savings claims.

A decision-support research programme comparing functions, mandates, deliverables and coordination arrangements within the UN peace and security pillar. The initial scope is the Department of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs (DPPA) and Department of Peace Operations (DPO), with adjacent offices and field operations included when the supplied sources support an explicit comparison.

## Decision question

Which activities could be shared, transferred, consolidated or redesigned **without reducing essential mandate delivery**?

## Evidence standard

- Separate **direct source statements** from **analytical inferences** and **unverified implementation status**.
- Similar subject matter **does not** establish duplication or waste.
- The 2027 documents are **proposals**, not actual costs, executable savings or proof of field implementation.
- Do not add across funding sources or mission budgets without checking boundaries and double counting.
- Treat UN80-announced integration as a **baseline to audit**, not a new opportunity to count again.
- Link each crosswalk finding to document section, numbered paragraph or table, and printed/PDF page.

## Primary sources

1. United Nations, **A/81/6 (Sect. 3)**, 8 May 2026, *Political affairs*, 134 pages.
2. United Nations, **A/81/6 (Sect. 5)**, 17 April 2026, *Peacekeeping operations*, 67 pages.

The two source documents were supplied by the project owner. Their text is **not paraphrased as a binding legal determination**. The underlying PDFs are not redistributed in this repository; see the source manifest and official document links in the forthcoming dataset.

## Repository organization

`data/`: documented functions, reported deliverables, evidence-paired crosswalk, UN80 register, candidate validation log.  
`docs/`: crosswalk interpretation, classification decisions, research limitations, work plan.  
`scripts/`: reproducible data-quality checks and release gates.

**No consolidation, personnel reduction or savings estimate has yet been independently validated.**
