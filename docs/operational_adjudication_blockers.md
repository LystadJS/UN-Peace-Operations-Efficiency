# Operational adjudication eligibility — X01–X21

**All 21 operational audit steps are `BLOCKED_NOT_EXECUTED`.** The [execution gate register](../data/adjudications/execution_gates.json) gives every task a separate phase, minimum evidence, custodian role, safeguard and next decision. These are explicit eligibility dispositions, not completed reconciliations or adjudications.

Each stream first reconciles underlying records, then evaluates a frozen feasible operational counterfactual, then receives independent adjudication. No later phase may bypass its predecessor. Public proposal values, reports of implementation and synthetic rehearsals cannot substitute for complete authorized records. The best GA-directed case remains unselected; a high public budget or apparent recovery exposure is not enough to select it.

The next decision is to confirm competent custodians and permitted intake, obtain complete records and assess substantive eligibility. No evidence request is treated as sent and no consent is presumed. User authorization for repository development and GitHub publication confers no UN institutional operational authority. Unknown operational outcomes and benefits remain unknown, not zero.

Future ledger entries require a complete structured F03 candidate/version history, legal gate sequence, mandatory checks and linked receipt identifiers, plus a reviewed history receipt identifying a controlled locator, SHA-256, reviewer, date and scope. State-name strings alone cannot satisfy this contract. The ledger's financial-review reference must match the F03 financial event. The validator checks declarations and link consistency only: neither a structured event nor a syntactically valid receipt authenticates underlying records, establishes reviewer competence or supplies institutional approval. Independent substantive review and a trusted prior snapshot remain necessary; passing synthetic fixtures confers no recognition authority.

Saving, avoidance and recovery entries require strictly positive finite exact-decimal strings. A measured zero belongs in the case assessment as a non-benefit finding. `NET_COST_INCREASE` uses a strictly negative amount under the baseline-minus-intervention convention and remains outside headline savings. Zero, negative positive-benefit claims, nonfinite values and non-string amounts fail validation and reconciliation.

I04 records the empty [verified benefit ledger](../data/portfolio/verified_benefit_ledger.json). I05 reconciles no recognized entries, excludes unverified cases, and preserves `null` totals. Its class-separated totals cannot combine recoveries, avoidance, capacity and savings. The headline includes only realized cashable savings; an empty headline is `NOT_ESTIMATED`, not a finding that there is no waste. Arithmetic checks do not authenticate financial receipts or approve an intervention.
