# Executive decision brief — extended UN peace-operations event census

**13 October 2026 | Source-linked mission, field and training records | No inferred savings**

## Decision

**Prioritize shared course governance and reproducible evidence, not elimination of related peacekeeping mandates.** Official MINUSCA, MONUSCO, UNMISS, UNISFA, Climate Security Mechanism, UNITAR and UNSSC materials extend the existing retrospective inventory. They identify delivered joint work, distinct training cohorts and complementary field service. The new institutional merger of UNITAR and UNSSC is **already authorized** under [General Assembly resolution A/RES/80/306](https://research.un.org/en/docs/unsystem/researchtraining), adopted **3 September 2026**, with UNICLI scheduled for **1 January 2027**. The existing DPO Integrated Training Service's peacekeeping training-standard responsibilities must remain distinguishable from the future institute's course delivery.

## Evidence safeguards and next audit gates

A **course listing** may establish advertised dates and stated fees, but it does not prove delivery, participant expenditure or a redundant class. One UNITAR Gako registration page contains inconsistent date fields (6–7 January in the title versus 6 January–7 February in the calendar), so its event date has not been silently corrected. The UNITAR Quarter I 2025 Division for Peace report describes **21 training events and 1,593 trained individuals across its wider programme**; those counts must not be added to the narrower curated UN peace-operations observations.

**To approve an actual operational change:** verify course identifiers, original curricula, common procurement/service clients, agency roles and delegated authority, and the distinct legally mandated outputs. A monetary savings claim additionally needs unique paid transaction identifiers, prospective service quality and net transition costs.
