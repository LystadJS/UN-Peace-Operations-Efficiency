# Conditional implementation and realization workflow

**No intervention has institutional approval in this project, and no live execution has occurred.** The [implementation gate register](../data/realization/implementation_gates.json) therefore contains an empty approved-intervention list and eight conditional step templates. Templates are not an execution record or permission to implement.

For each genuinely approved intervention, create eight separately reviewable tasks:

1. Approve the intervention charter and competent owner, scope and rollback authority.
2. Establish the prospective measurement register and information handling.
3. Freeze the feasible baseline and attribution rules before outcome inspection.
4. Execute the specifically authorized intervention with a change log and rollback route.
5. Measure financial and operational outcomes over the approved observation window.
6. Reconcile transferred work, provider cost, internal allocations, retained work and liabilities.
7. Independently assess benefit realization and required operational safeguards.
8. Decide whether to scale, revise, retain the current arrangement or roll back.

Before step one, require actual institutional approval, accountable owner confirmation, permitted records, accepted safeguards and an `IMPLEMENTATION_READY` evidence receipt. A savings claim additionally requires actual `FINANCIALLY_VALIDATED` history; operational advancement alone cannot supply it. Observation windows and numeric stop thresholds require approved case baselines and must not be invented from public budget estimates.

Internal charge reductions are eliminated against provider credits and cannot become whole-UN savings. Already embedded reforms belong to their own realization baseline. Capacity released remains distinct from cash release; recovery and avoidance remain separate from headline realized savings. Adverse effects and credible zero or negative findings must be retained alongside favorable outcomes.

No restricted record or live action is authorized by these repository templates. Actual operational adjudications and realization remain blocked until their substantive gates are met.
