# Commander's decision memo — I06

**Advance the three priority evidence requests for competent-owner review; retain current operations until authorized evidence establishes a better arrangement.** The project now has a reproducible public research and validation framework for identifying avoidable resource use and improving peace operations while protecting delivery. It has not completed an operational efficiency audit, validated a monetary benefit or established UN-wide reform compliance. Publication and software validation supply no institutional authority to change operations.

The portfolio contains [14 screening case packets](../data/cases/) covering shared-service charges, asset reuse, Haiti support, GA-directed controls, staffing transitions, training, regional workflows and existing joint structures. Their documented mechanisms justify investigation, not a finding of duplication or waste. There are **zero completed substantive operational adjudications**, against the [completion criterion](desired_end_state.md) of at least three bounded audits across at least two service types. The minimum top-tier financial adjudication is also unmet. Benefits remain not estimated: the [verified-benefit ledger](../data/portfolio/verified_benefit_ledger.json) is empty and monetary totals are unknown, rather than measured zero.

The [reform baseline](../data/reforms/baseline_release.json) is partial. Selected adopted GA80 provisions, ACABQ advice and SG proposals have distinct classifications; implementation and applicability gaps remain. The GA81 review has not authenticated an in-scope adopted operative requirement. Its empty action extraction does not establish that no applicable GA81 obligation exists. Reform alignment must be demonstrated paragraph by paragraph, with competent authority, affected function, conditions and period established before describing a change as required by reform.

The next decision is whether competent custodians will accept the proposed bounded validation scope and approve access, handling and burden. These proposed responsibilities remain unaccepted; all [request statuses](../data/pilots/request_status.json) remain unsent and no response has been obtained:

Prepare these requests under the [secure-intake boundary](secure_evidence_intake.md). Dispatch, collection and institutional engagement require the appropriate explicit authorization; this memo neither sends requests nor assigns institutional owners. Agree comparable observation periods, extraction completeness, data minimization, collection burden, materiality and prospective mandate, safety, readiness, quality, timeliness and transition thresholds before examining intervention outcomes. Unknown burden and unset thresholds remain open dependencies, not assumed acceptable values.

Once usable records and approvals exist, reconcile each baseline, test a feasible alternative and adjudicate the result with a reviewer other than the case author. Include local work that remains, work received elsewhere, recurring external cost, transition expense and residual liabilities. Eliminate internal transfers once and exclude embedded reforms and prior closures from newly attributable benefits. Accept adverse, null and retain-current-process outcomes. No monetary finding may be called savings before actual `FINANCIALLY_VALIDATED` history and supporting substantive review; later operational states cannot bypass this gate.

No operational cut, staffing action, service consolidation, mandate change, live reporting trial or course-watch restart is recommended by this release. If an intervention subsequently passes the operational and financial gates, the competent owner must separately accept an implementation charter, funded receiving capability, measurement period and stop or rollback rules. The desired end state remains demonstrable improvement in peacekeeping delivery and defensible compliance with applicable adopted reforms, with no quota for positive savings. Evidence-unavailable dispositions cannot substitute for that result.
