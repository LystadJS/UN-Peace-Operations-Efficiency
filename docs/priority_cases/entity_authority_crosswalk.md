# Scoped entity authority crosswalk

As of 23 October 2026, this overlay links five existing canonical entities to the C01–C03 public-source receipts. The [machine-readable crosswalk](../../data/priority_cases/entity_authority_crosswalk.json) preserves exact canonical IDs, evidence limits and source-packet hashes. It does not replace [the frozen 94-entity register](../../data/portfolio/entities.json): its unknown lifecycle and mandate fields remain unchanged, and the other 89 entities have not been reviewed here.

Council decisions establish the relevant mandated functions and periods; they do not substitute for GA appropriations, applicable financial delegations, recipient designation or signed service and transfer records. ACABQ advice and SG budget proposals retain their separate authority classes. Financial periods, legal mandate periods and reported operating milestones must remain separate when comparing these entities.

All three cases remain SCREENING_CANDIDATE and BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE. This overlay creates no financial finding, accepted owner, approval or operational action. The source review is bounded; it is not a complete search for later amendments or corrigenda. Repository source-packet hashes identify the inspected receipts and do not claim hashes of remote final documents.
