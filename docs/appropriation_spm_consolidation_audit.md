# GA mission financing reconciliation and SPM consolidation audit

**Updated 8 October 2026 · Status: 6/6 GA mission appropriations numerically verified; 2027 SPM addenda financial extraction remains open.**

## Executive finding

The six uploaded adopted General Assembly financing resolutions, independently cross-checked against the Secretary-General's **[approved-resource note A/C.5/80/20](https://docs.un.org/en/A/C.5/80/20)**, authorize **US$4,028.1880 million in mission-maintenance gross requirements** compared with **US$4,120.9507 million in the original Secretary-General's proposals**. The **−US$92.7627 million** difference represents a budget decision (**2.25% below request**), **not** identified DPPA–DPO duplicated costs or incremental reform savings.

The same six General Assembly resolutions appropriate **US$4,442.1194 million inclusive of** the mission-maintenance budgets plus **US$325.0875 million in support-account shares**, **US$48.2819 million for the UN Logistics Base (UNLB)**, and **US$40.5620 million for the Regional Service Centre in Entebbe (RSCE)**. The inclusive figure is **not** comparable to the original mission-maintenance-only requests.

## Operative General Assembly mandates for audit follow-up

- **UNIFIL** — [A/RES/80/282, paragraphs 12–15](https://docs.un.org/en/A/RES/80/282): separation costs, site closure and environmental handover, and personnel safety during withdrawal; test intermission agreements with UNTSO/UNDOF/UNSCO before revising shared support.
- **UNMISS** — [A/RES/80/283, paragraph 9 and Annex](https://docs.un.org/en/A/RES/80/283): verify 91 approved civilian abolishments, especially 53 electoral and nine rule-of-law/security positions, without counting already adopted changes as new savings.
- **UNISFA** — [A/RES/80/275, paragraphs 9–11](https://docs.un.org/en/A/RES/80/275): review centralized translation capacity, underutilized assets, and accountability for transferred **MINUSMA** assets; see also paragraphs 12 and 15 on protection/fleet capability.
- **UNSOS** — [A/RES/80/285, paragraphs 3–4](https://docs.un.org/en/A/RES/80/285): track liquidity-related delivery impact and the requested progress on fuel-contractor claims recovery. No quantified recoverable amount is established in the resolution.
- **MONUSCO** — [A/RES/80/278, paragraphs 9–11](https://docs.un.org/en/A/RES/80/278): strengthen controls over irregular medical-insurance claims and preserve ceasefire and emergency-medical readiness.
- **MINUSCA** — [A/RES/80/276, paragraph 9](https://docs.un.org/en/A/RES/80/276): capture and apply relevant election-support lessons.

The [15 item operative-controls register](../data/ga_adopted_directives.csv) links these duties to existing crosswalk investigations and preserves unquantified items as **not yet attributable**.

### Appropriation is not the same as apportionment or expenditure

The resolutions authorize **appropriation** for 1 July 2026–30 June 2027. Five of the missions have **two assessed-apportionment periods**, with later segments dependent on additional Security Council decisions; UNIFIL has one appropriation-apportionment period for the full fiscal year encompassing cessation and withdrawal. The [11-row apportionment schedule](../data/ga_apportionment_schedule.csv) records those legal conditions. Staff-assessment equalization, unencumbered credits, unpaid assessments, and other revenue are financing adjustments, not amounts saved by departmental consolidation.

## SPM 2027 addenda and unresolved discrepancies

These seven uploads were **2026/27 peacekeeping GA resolutions and the approved-resource note**, not **A/81/6 (Sect.3)/Add.1–4** for the **2027 calendar-year SPM proposals**. SPM addenda financial reconciliation remains **0/4**. A/81/6 (Sect.3) paragraph 3.VI.1 p113 gives a *provisional* **$423.5445m** regular-budget aggregate for **36 missions**. Its extrabudgetary narrative figure **$37.7001m** differs from Annex IV's **$32.7405m**, an unresolved **$4.9596m** difference. Neither is a realized saving.

The [2027 SPM tracker](../data/spm_2027_addenda.csv) remains open, with cluster reviews identified through the [ACABQ official index](https://www.un.org/ga/acabq/en/reports). **2027 calendar-year SPM requests must not be combined as if they were approved 2026/27 July–June peacekeeping appropriations.**
