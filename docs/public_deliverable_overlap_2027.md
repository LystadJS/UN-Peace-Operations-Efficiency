# 2027 deliverable-level overlap audit — UNOWAS/UNOCA, UNAMA/UNRCCA, DPPA/DPO and training

**Public proposal tables only | As of 8 October 2026 | 32 paired investigations**

## Phase 9 clarification

The latest [Cluster III SG and ACABQ main-report mapping](acabq_2027_clusterIII_review.md) confirms all nine 2027 mission requests and maps relevant general service/reporting standards; it does **not** identify identical funded reports or an ACABQ Cluster III cut. The 32 output pairs below remain screening hypotheses.

## Bottom line

**No pair of identical, separately charged 2027 final deliverables has yet been proven.** The four comparisons do identify testable opportunities to harmonize source data, meeting preparation, travel scheduling, curricula, shared procurement and administrative support. Similar quantities in a UN proposed budget cannot establish duplicated expenditure without unique report, mission, meeting or course identifiers.

The [32-row source-linked master crosswalk](../data/public_deliverable_crosswalk_2027.csv) contains eight paired items for each comparison, 2027 planned values, table/page anchors, official document links, documentary classification, and a specific adjudication question. **Missing counts are explicitly not zero** and should not be converted to zero to simulate a difference.

## 1. UNOWAS versus UNOCA

Source: **A/81/6 (Sect.3)/Add.4**, [UNOWAS Table 7 pp.17–18](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%203%29/Add.4#page=17) and [UNOCA Table 35 p.51](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%203%29/Add.4#page=51).

| Planned 2027 measure | UNOWAS | UNOCA | Primary distinction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Reports to Security Council | 2 | 2 | Different geographic and political scope |
| Security Council meetings supported | 2 | 2 | Same institutional recipient does not mean same meeting or work |
| Training/workshop days | 15 | 13 | Regional peace/security versus Central African programming |
| Field-monitoring visits | 20 | 20 | Visits to different State populations; some cross-border themes |
| Technical issue papers | 6 | Not separately counted | UNOCA narrative material is not a numeric zero |
| Standing Advisory Committee ministerial meetings | Not separately counted | 10 | UNOCA-specific regional committee |

**Documented relation:** Add.4 para.22 p.14 states that UNOWAS and UNOCA coordinate on transregional issues, including Lake Chad, farmer–herder conflict, climate/security and Gulf of Guinea maritime matters. That supports a **common-source analytical layer and occasional shared preparation**, not closure of independent regional diplomacy offices. ECOWAS-facing and ECCAS-facing responsibilities must remain distinguished.

**Public next study:** retrieve actual Secretary-General report symbols and Security Council meeting IDs in 2025–2026; mark authoring teams, overlapping data, unique political audience, and joint field itinerary. Only shared purchased services or separable production costs may become later financial candidates.

**Public next study:** map the actual published Council documents, cross-border early-warning topics and planned workshop calendars; isolate platform/licence and travel savings hypotheses separately from protected field functions.

## 3. DPPA–DPO shared regional political-operational desks

Primary 2027 proposed programme tables: [DPPA Section 3, Table 3.I.4 p.16](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%203%29#page=16), [DPO Section 5, Table 5.I.5 p.11](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%205%29#page=11), and [DPPA Security Council substantive procedural outputs Table 3.I.7 p.25](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%203%29#page=25).

| Planned 2027 indicator | DPPA | DPO | Comparability limitation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Secretary-General reports to the Security Council | 50 peace/security reports | 21 peacekeeping reports | Possibly share brief inputs; **different report populations** |
| SC meetings or consultations | 80 | 34 | DPPA count includes consultations and subsidiary work; DPO peacekeeping meetings |
| Parliamentary or SG outputs of different type | 16 General Assembly peace/security reports | 10 SG-to-SC letters | Not the same document class |
| Regional institutions in reported advisory reach | More than 24 | 5 | Distinct good offices versus peacekeeping operational partnerships |
| DPPA mediation support / DPO transition planning | Approx. 22 SPMs | Not separately counted | Different mission portfolios and products |

Both proposals explicitly describe **existing/unified regional political-operational structures** under UN80. The issue is not whether there are two differently named Headquarters desks on paper. The relevant question is whether **two separately costed teams actually produce the same source dossier, Council briefing input or trip itinerary** within that new structure. DPPA's Section 3 also separately counts 400 Council procedural meetings in its Security Council affairs subprogramme; do **not** characterize those legally mandated procedural services as duplicates of DPO's 34 substantive peacekeeping meetings.

**Public next study:** compile unique 2025–2027 UN document symbols, Council meeting records, publicly attributed leads, organigrams and written workplans; review actual production lineage, not just report totals. A financed redundancy determination would require post IDs and effort allocation unavailable in public documents.

## 4. DPPA versus DPO training functions

Primary tables: DPPA [Table 3.I.4 p.16](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%203%29#page=16), DPO [military Table 5.I.9 p.15](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%205%29#page=15), [police/justice Table 5.I.11 p.21](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%205%29#page=21) and [policy/training Table 5.I.14 p.27](https://docs.un.org/en/A/81/6%20%28Sect.%205%29#page=27).

| Planned 2027 training calendar | Days | What is covered |
|---|---:|---|
| DPPA Subprogramme 1 | 150 | Preventive diplomacy, peacemaking, peacebuilding and related regional training |
| DPO Subprogramme 2 — Military | 136 | Predeployment, exercises, military policy, intelligence, gender and tactical readiness |
| DPO Subprogramme 3 — Police/justice | 50 | Police readiness, correctional/justice functions, mine action and accountability |
| DPO Subprogramme 4 — Policy/evaluation/training | 360 | Curriculum/trainer assistance, leadership, mission advisers and policy support |
| DPO Subprogramme 4 curriculum/training-of-trainers subset | 312 | **Within** 360, not 312 extra days |

These are **planned activity days, not necessarily distinct attendees, course modules, rooms or expenses**. Adding them up does not identify shared financial overhead. Military exercises, safeguarding responsibilities, police/corrections qualifications, and Member-State Security Council orientation are not interchangeable with political mediation training.

**Potentially feasible administrative changes:** a consolidated schedule and venue/vendor procurement, shared trainer credential registry, standard course evaluation instruments, and common learning platform where security permits. Proposals to eliminate substantive courses require a higher evidentiary threshold than sharing scheduling or purchasing support.

## Proposed release standard

All [32 output comparisons](../data/public_deliverable_crosswalk_2027.csv) are **documentary screening only**. For a paired activity to be upgraded to *verified duplication*, the analyst must have: (a) same actual unique output identifier or precisely equivalent deliverable, (b) same recipient and time period, (c) identifiable separate work or supplier charge, (d) legal ability to consolidate, and (e) preserved performance and security conditions. Count-value similarity alone is not sufficient.

**Decision:** Reuse a common source event log, field-trip identity, training materials where mandates and cohorts permit, and controlled briefing-input packets. Preserve distinct Council reports and specialist training. No independently valued duplicate charge has been established. Review [11 ranked reuse tests](../data/retrospective_2025_2026_reuse_decisions.csv) and [match records](../data/retrospective_2025_2026_matches.csv) before any operational reorganization or monetary saving claim.

**Further public work:** Obtain actual versioned course materials and the full Council report sections where only index metadata was accessible; expand this purposive sample into a systematic 2025–2026 collection using canonical document and event identifiers. No restricted transaction files are needed until the separate cost audit.
