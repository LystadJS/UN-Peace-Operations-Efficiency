# C05 — UNISFA stewardship of MINUSMA-transferred and underused assets

**BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE · SCREENING_CANDIDATE.** Retained evidence cutoff: 8 October 2026; assembled 23 October. This dossier does not refresh governing texts or grant operational authority.

Retained A/RES/80/275 paragraphs 10–11 requests underutilized asset assessment and accountable management of MINUSMA transfers; paragraphs 12 and 15 protect security and fleet capability. Serial-level transfer and utilization records are absent.

Test whether usable transferred items can meet approved demand and avoid an otherwise necessary acquisition, or whether underuse reflects condition, access, maintenance or a justified readiness reserve. Repair, reassign or dispose of individually assessed assets under approved authority; retain justified operational reserves and protective capacity.

Assess each serial over remaining service life. Carrying value and donor write-off are accounting transfers, not cash benefits. Net any avoidable external purchase against transport, storage, repair, spares, insurance and disposal proceeds; do not overlap the translation case or general mission budget.

Low observed use alone would not establish avoidable cost: mission security, seasonality and serviceability can require reserve assets.

Required safeguards:

- Vehicle serviceability and emergency availability
- Counter-UAS and personnel protection within mandate
- Safe accountable custody and authorized disposal

Minimum evidence:

- Donor MINUSMA release and UNISFA signed receipt with serial IDs and dates
- Inventory/GL postings, ownership, condition, location and remaining life
- Utilization logs, downtime, maintenance invoices and reserve rationale
- Approved recipient demand, canceled requisition/PO and net transfer lifecycle costs

Custodian roles to confirm: UNISFA property management and fleet engineering; MINUSMA liquidation asset custodian role; UNISFA procurement, finance and security planners. The accountable owner is unknown and no request has been sent.
