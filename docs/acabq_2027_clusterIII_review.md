Core conclusions relevant to missions:

- **Efficiency measurement (Chapter I, para. 10):** The Secretariat reported no consolidated registry for verified entity-level realized efficiencies. ACABQ recommends distinguishing **planned and achieved** gains and recording implementation costs, productivity and service quality; lack of expenditure under liquidity constraints is not evidence of savings.
- **Shared-service redesign (paras. 79–93):** Establish consistent baseline service catalogs, measurable workload and unit-cost comparators, client accountability and transparent local retained-functions exceptions. This informs BINUH/UNSOH and UNOWAS/CNMC audit design without establishing duplicate expense.
- **Training (paras. 68–69):** Administrative training and course procurement may be rationalized but cannot be financially costed from workshop *day counts*. The Secretariat reported lacking activity-based training expense tracking.
- **Assets and relocation (paras. 7, 72–76):** Use cost/benefit tests covering implementation and recurring charges for relocations, and strengthen lifecycle management and asset redeployments. Applies especially to UNSCOL, UNSMIL and Colombia.
- **Regional offices and envoys (Section 3, para. II.7):** Review roles of DPPA regional divisions, liaison offices and envoys, coordinated with DPO and field capacity. The same Lake Chad or Afghanistan information may support several **distinct** formally mandated outputs.
- **Personnel and accountability (paras. 14, 40, 44–52):** Distinguish actual workforce changes from unfilled positions, authorized abolishments, transfers, grade changes and organizational-chart projections; do not count one person or funded role twice.

The [51-row mission-to-framework crosswalk](../data/acabq_2027_clusterIII_crosscutting_map.csv) records **analytical applicability only**. It **does not claim** the Committee individually ordered any of the nine missions to make the mapped changes.

2. **Cluster I and II ACABQ advice** (−$182.5k net and $0 change, respectively) remains valid. No **Cluster III** new ACABQ adjustment can be derived from those reports.
3. The separate **$3.2591m RSCE SPM share** is inside the SG's $422.671m overall request. It is not a mission-specific Cluster III budget line or a saving.
4. A purely mechanical illustration of a **partial-advice carry-forward** totals **$422.4885m** only if **all missing Cluster III and RSCE advisory adjustments are assumed zero**. This is a *scenario*, **not ACABQ's recommended total** and not a defensible projection of what the Committee or GA will decide.
