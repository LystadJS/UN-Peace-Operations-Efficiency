# GA financing resolutions, special political missions, and consolidation audit — Phase 3

**Status: source reconciliation partially complete, numerical appropriation reconciliation open.**
Updated 8 October 2026. Public documentary evidence only. No savings certified.

## Executive findings

The six 2026/27 mission budget proposals have been matched to the General Assembly financing resolutions adopted **30 June 2026**. The six resolutions and the Fifth Committee draft identifiers are verified against the [official decisions register](https://www.un.org/en/ga/fifth/80/resdec80.shtml). However, I could not independently retrieve and read the **operative dollar appropriation amounts** in these resolutions or in the cross-mission approved-resource statement A/C.5/80/20. The linked source register therefore explicitly records **not verified**, with no fabricated proposal-to-approval difference.

The 2027 special political mission (SPM) budget belongs to a different calendar and financing process. Its **$423.5445 million** regular-budget request for **36 missions** is provisional, not an appropriation. Addenda 1–4 appear in the official [81st session work programme](https://www.un.org/en/ga/fifth/81/C5_81_0m_pow.pdf); the [ACABQ register](https://www.un.org/ga/acabq/en/reports) confirms addenda for thematic clusters I and II and mentions the chapeau. Detailed addendum financial tables and cluster III have not been extracted.

The practical output is a **12-candidate ranked audit queue**, where each published amount describes a *funding perimeter* around a question, never a demonstrated redundant or recoverable cost.

## D. Strong historical evidence for transition procurement risk

The UN [Board of Auditors, A/80/5 (Vol. I)](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4090309/files/A_80_5_%28Vol._I%29-EN.pdf) records two cases that help design current checks:

- **UNAMI (paras. 199–201, pp.54–55):** The Board flagged **$11.80m in purchase orders** with scheduled delivery dates after a May 2024 drawdown decision and identified insufficient documented assessment of whether those purchases should be cancelled or retained. The Administration offered explanations concerning some asset purchases. **This amount is not an established loss or available 2026/27 saving.**
- **Somalia / UNSOM–UNTMIS (paras. 127–134, pp.43–45):** An office project with an **estimated $8.50m cost** had advanced before changed transition requirements led the mission to discontinue it and explore its transfer to another UN entity. **Estimated construction cost is not automatically avoidable.**

For UNIFIL/UNTSO and other transitions, these cases support a specific control: each purchase order and asset should have an explicit *retain, cancel, transfer, or close* decision, along with its residual liability, funding source, receiving entity and protection implications.

## E. Reconciliation gates / controls

1. **Authoritative GA resolution text**: six operative appropriations and A/C.5/80/20; source-page anchors; reconciliation of gross appropriation, credits and assessed shares.
2. **Complete SPM 2027 addenda**: mission roster, actual cluster subtotals, common costs, currency/fiscal basis and cross-check of the SPM extrabudgetary discrepancy.
3. **Incremental cost method**: subtract prior adopted cuts, vacancies and planned closures; distinguish per-fund cost objects and existing shared-unit charges; account for one-off transition liabilities.
4. **Operations and legal gate**: distinct mandates, unique beneficiary and service IDs, Security Council operational requirements and host-state constraints.
5. **Independent review**: the final recommendation must record approving authority, implementation deadline, maintainable outputs and sensitivity range; not only a gross budget amount.

**Audit status:** GA authority mapping **6/6**; independently verified adopted-dollar variances **0/6**; SPM schedule **4/4**, detailed addendum financial tables **0/4**; validation rankings **12**. Therefore this is a **partial source and boundary reconciliation**, not a completed appropriations reconciliation.
