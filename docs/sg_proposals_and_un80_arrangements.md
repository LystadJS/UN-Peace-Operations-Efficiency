Only indexed excerpts were available; direct PDF retrieval returned 403. Neither excerpt establishes plenary adoption or completed implementation.

A [July informal plenary briefing transcript](https://transcripts.un.org/en/asset/k1u/k1ujz9s3tb), ASG Peacebuilding and Peace Support at 9:07–22:57, reports PBPSO establishment in January 2026 and describes consolidation as continuing. The page explicitly identifies its transcript as automatic speech recognition and not an official UN record or document. Its reference to Member-State approval is a management account requiring an independently linked adopted instrument. This supports `IMPLEMENTATION_REPORTED` for U02 only, rather than verified institutional authority, complete operational validation, or benefit realization.

For every arrangement, obtain the relevant adopted or delegated authority, dated organizational and unique-post records, workplans and service outputs, and comparable full-system cost records. Preserve safeguards and test the counterfactual before any financial finding. U02's combined OAMP/PBPS allocation cannot be assigned to PBPS alone; U01's DPO support-account slice is not its total cost. Existing joint structures and already budgeted changes belong in the counterfactual. All eleven financial-validation statuses remain `NOT_PERFORMED`, with benefits `NOT_ESTIMATED` and amounts null. Null is not zero.

Apply the [source-authority](source_authority_standard.md), [cost-accounting](cost_accounting_standard.md), and [candidate advancement](candidate_advancement.md) standards. These registers are source-treatment controls and do not advance any candidate state or authorize operational changes.
