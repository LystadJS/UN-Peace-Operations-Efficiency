## Decision requested

**Determine whether a specific factual-support control is missing from each existing workflow. Retain current arrangements where equivalent controls already operate.** Only a documented gap should lead to consideration of a bounded, non-displacing shadow under the existing protocol. This memo does not request an office merger, new coordinating unit, platform, synchronized deadline, staff reduction or budget cut. Each case can be retained, revised, declined or considered separately. [1][2]

The proposed intervention is narrow: a source-attributed record of selected facts, their qualifications and versions, feeding **separate** reports, briefings, political analysis and clearance. Public evidence establishes cooperation and technical representability; it does **not** establish avoidable internal preparation. Treat the register's open questions as questions, not 16 proven defects. [1][2]

## 4. Comparison, safeguards and later financial questions

Compare the **same factual-support work slice**, not a whole SG report with a short fact sheet. Retain normal, shadow, common, setup and evaluation effort; common work counts once in actual trial burden. Missing/estimated time, unequal tasks or hidden dependence on a normal draft preclude a complete-process contrast. Keep factual-support and boundary-preservation quality strata separate. A faster materially incorrect output must not advance. [4]

Stop affected shadow work for authority/security breaches, threatened normal deadlines, material misattribution or invented stages, or exhausted effort ceilings. Stop the quantitative comparison for unequal inputs or unreliable capture; required normal production continues. No automatic restart or retrospective ceiling increase is permitted. Live logs, signatures and private evidence belong only in locally approved storage. [4]

**GF01–GF02 are later gates, not demands for financial records at initial review.** A parallel shadow does not demonstrate labor displaced. Only a separately authorized replacement test can establish whether a named task disappears without hidden transfer. Any subsequent cash claim requires cancelable cost objects net of operating and transition costs. No staff-hour or monetary estimate, target or new ranking is supplied. [4]
