# Initial findings — DPPA / DPO 2027 crosswalk

**Status:** preliminary source-grounded comparison, 8 October 2026. **No validated net savings.**

## Bottom line

The two 2027 proposals confirm joint structures and planned coordination. They do **not** establish that comparable-looking mandates create waste. The initial exercise yields 39 source-paired comparisons, including 9 potential functional overlaps needing operational evidence. The strongest opportunity for immediate analytic progress is an **implementation and cost-allocation audit of reforms already described under UN80**, followed by reporting, training, information management, and partner-facing workflows.

### Specific pilots

**Southern Lebanon.** DPO §5.II.20 p.45 describes UNIFIL's December 2026 cessation date and the drawdown/withdrawal through December 2027, with ongoing UNTSO responsibilities. The DPO UNTSO deliverables at Table 5.II.4 p.46 explicitly list inter-mission support agreements with **UNSCO and UNDOF**. This is a concrete baseline of existing shared arrangements; incremental opportunities require service agreements, transport/medical/communications costs and mission transition records. See CW026–CW027 and C12.

**Central African Republic.** DPO §5.I.18 p.8 describes MINUSCA support to DDR and elections; DPPA Annex III pp.128–129 identifies proposed PBF programmes on justice, governance, local conflict and peacebuilding. This establishes related activity in one setting, not a duplicated project or cost. Validate implementing partners, geographies, recipients and cost objects. See CW028, CW037 and C13.

**UN–African Union partnership.** UNOAU §3.IV.4 pp.84–85 and DPO §5.I.2(g) pp.3–4 both engage AU peace and security functions. UNOAU Annex I p.114 proposes an internal service merger, but **UNOAU §3.IV.11 p.86 states no substantive adjustments arising from UN80**. The service merger must not be relabelled as a documented UN80 outcome. See CW020–CW021, CW031 and C08.

## Cost and source-quality exceptions

- **SPM extrabudgetary figures conflict:** DPPA §3.VI.1 p.113 gives $37.7001 million; Section 3 Annex IV p.133 gives $32.7405 million. Neither is silently substituted for the other.
- **PBPS Office change requires reconciliation:** §3.I.129 p.58 narrates a decrease of $14,400; Table 3.I.36 p.59 shows a decrease of $41.4 thousand.
- **PBPS Office regular-budget figure includes grants:** $50 million of grants and contributions must not be classified as staffing or other administrative overhead.
- **DPO departmental budgets omit many field operations:** the departmental $191.51 million total from Table 5.I.18 p.30 is not the combined field-mission peacekeeping appropriation.
- **Budget types and annual outputs are heterogeneous:** activity counts and programme funding are not directly comparable until normalised by purpose, audience, year, and accounting boundary.

All open issues are tracked in [quality_issues.csv](../data/quality_issues.csv). All findings are **screening conclusions**; no line has a validated financial savings estimate.
