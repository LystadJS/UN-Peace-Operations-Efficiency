# Executive readout — 2025–2026 retrospective UN peace-operations overlap

**8 October 2026 | Public-document evidence only**

**Decision conclusion:** Preserve the already functioning joint products and standardize how the UN reuses their underlying information, events and administrative purchases. **Do not recommend merging mandated regional reports, specialist courses or units solely because their outputs share topics or personnel.** Public sources reveal joint delivery, not independently priced double spending.

### Evidence collected

|---|---:|

| Adjudicated source-linked comparison records | **36** |

| Verified new net cost savings | **Not estimated** |

### Most actionable established reuse

- **UNOWAS–UNOCA:** one January 2025 Nigeria high-level mission and one February 2025 Dakar farmer–herder workshop, both described in separate regional reports. Adopt **shared canonical source/event logs** and avoid counting nested visits as extra missions.
- **UNAMA–UNRCCA:** joint April 2025 academy and February 2026 climate capacity-building in cooperation with DPPA and UNSSC. Standardize shared course resources, but distinguish **organizer, host, instructor and attendee**.
- **DPPA–DPO:** jointly produced mission guidance and peace-operations review, plus Council appearances by integrated regional leadership. Attribute **one joint delivered product** per report or meeting.
- **Training modules:** UNRCCA/UNOCT Tashkent and Astana OSINT/AI courses served different cohorts. They may reuse materials and procurement; they are **not duplicate courses**.

### No reduction supported by current evidence

S/2025/187 and S/2025/342 mention the same Dakar workshop, but each is a different mandated Council report. Similarly, UNOCA's S/PV.10060 and UNOWAS's S/PV.10073 are separate Council meetings, not duplicate public briefings. UNAMA's Afghanistan country reports are not interchangeable with UNRCCA's regional diplomatic engagements. Climate-security policing and political/peacebuilding training have different audiences and safety requirements.

**Recommended next action:** Establish an **event–source–output identity register** for common transregional data and administrative coordination. Commission a targeted vendor/venue/course comparison once authorized service costs are available; financial savings remain *not estimated*.
