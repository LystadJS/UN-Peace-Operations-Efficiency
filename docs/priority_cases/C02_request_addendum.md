**Draft, not sent; collection authority and owner acceptance remain unobtained.** This [addendum](../../data/priority_cases/C02_request_addendum.json) extends [E02](../../data/evidence_requests/E02_assets.json) without duplicating its asset-event contract or requesting actual records into Git.

Fresh review of [resolution 2790](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=S/RES/2790(2025)&t=pdf) distinguishes the general safe-transfer direction in OP9 from the specific recipient and release approvals still needed. Continuing withdrawal protection and subsequent liquidation requirements under OP6–7 constrain resource release.

Five additional linked evidence tables address the remaining decision uncertainties:

| Addition | Minimum decision evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Specific authority and custody | Competent financial/property approval naming the designated recipient, scope, effective period, donor release and recipient acceptance. |
| Asset/lot to recipient need | Private serialized/lot mapping, dated inventory, condition/life or expiry, approved unmet need and quantity/function substitution. |
| Procurement disposition | Existing requisition/PO line, cancellation chronology, irreversible commitment and the supported external-purchase counterfactual. |
| Protected capability reservations | Security, medical, enabling, withdrawal and liquidation holds or competent clearance, with minimum necessary operational detail. |
| Net and residual cost components | Unique transport/refurbishment/installation/security/medical/guard/storage/operating and disposal-proceeds components, linked to the existing aggregate cost fields. |

Reuse `asset_token` and `procurement_requirement_token` from E02. Custodian-held crosswalks connect them to actual serial/VIN, lot and procurement identifiers in approved restricted systems. Quantities must reconcile to both available assets and unmet need. Component costs reconcile to their parent aggregates and the shared Lebanon-transition pool; they are not new amounts to add to existing totals.

Periods, extraction burden, numerical safeguards and actual values remain unset. An incomplete cost population, ambiguous recipient authority or protected-capability hold blocks operational release.
