# C06 — UNSOS recovery for fuel contractor nonperformance

**BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE · SCREENING_CANDIDATE.** Retained evidence cutoff: 8 October 2026; assembled 23 October. This dossier does not refresh governing texts or grant operational authority.

The retained A/RES/80/285 paragraph 4 extraction calls for action and reporting on delayed recovery of economic losses from fuel contractor nonperformance. Paragraph 3 requires attention to mandate impacts of liquidity contingencies. No contract claim amount or settled receipt is retained.

Test whether a documented enforceable loss has been correctly assessed, pursued and collected, and whether collection delays arise from missing evidence, dispute, insolvency or administrative processing. An authorized contract-claim reconciliation and recovery workplan, with dispute escalation and continuity of fuel supply; retain legitimate disputed amounts pending adjudication.

Recovery is distinct from cost avoidance and recurring savings. Reconcile gross claim, credits, offsets, collection expense, taxes, settlement and receipt once. Do not count receivable recognition as cash collection or combine recovered past loss with future procurement reductions.

The resolution follow-up establishes an investigation and reporting basis, not a recoverable amount, enforceability determination or contractor culpability finding by this project.

Required safeguards:

- Fuel availability for UNSOS and supported operations
- Contractual due process and competent legal authority
- No public disclosure of privileged dispute or vendor-sensitive material

Minimum evidence:

- Signed contract, amendments, performance obligations and remedy clauses
- Delivery/quality tests, nonperformance events, validated loss calculation and claim notices
- Dispute status, approvals, limitation dates and settlement terms
- Receivable ledger, credit notes, offsets, actual bank/treasury receipt and recovery costs
