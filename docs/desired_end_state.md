# Desired end state — public assessment amendment

**Controlling primary-completion amendment | 23 October 2026 | Prior operational criteria retained below, unmet**

The primary deliverable is a source-backed public assessment of peacekeeping efficiency and reform accountability: what the available evidence supports, what remains conditional or unknown, and what decision or evidence would resolve each question. This route addresses the practical likelihood that operational records will remain unavailable. It does not certify waste, UN compliance, institutional approval or realized benefits.

The [public assessment standard](public_assessment_standard.md) and [frozen scope](../data/public_assessment/scope.json) control primary publication and completion. The initial scope covers detailed public assessments of C01–C03 and transparent screening/triage of all fourteen historical parent cases; the other eleven are explicitly outside detailed assessment, not removed from the denominator. Selected reform evidence cannot stand for exhaustive UNGA80/81 coverage.

Completion requires evidence-qualified answers and dispositions, financial-class separation, independent scoped review, a reproducible leadership brief, closure or explicit limitation of feasible public-research gaps, and reopening triggers. A supported concern, conditional opportunity, unresolved question or reasoned decision to retain the current process can satisfy the public method. No positive monetary result is required. Definitions and acceptance criteria are in the controlling standard; this amendment does not declare them achieved.

Operational validation is an optional deeper route when authorized records become available. Its accepted owners, actual baselines, operational audits and implementation/realization gates remain mandatory for those respective claims, but are not prerequisites for publishing or completing the public assessment. Public completion is never described as operational realization. F03 advancement states and the prohibition on savings language before FINANCIALLY_VALIDATED remain unchanged.

## Retained optional operational path — original F01 text

The original 22 October standard follows unchanged. Its mandatory gate language applies to the optional decision-grade operational and realization path. It no longer defines the primary public-assessment publication/completion gate. None of its unmet conditions is retroactively fulfilled or waived for an operational claim.

# F01 — Desired end state and portfolio completion criteria

**Project standard | Defined 22 October 2026 | Normative design, not a finding of implementation or savings**

## Purpose

UN Peace Operations Efficiency will provide a decision-grade portfolio of specific improvements to peacekeeping functions and services. It will identify avoidable resource consumption and feasible efficiency improvements, protect operational effectiveness, and connect each recommendation to applicable adopted UNGA80 and UNGA81 requirements. The project succeeds through defensible decisions, including decisions to retain effective arrangements or withhold judgment where evidence is insufficient. It has no required positive savings target.

A reviewer must be able to follow each intervention from an adopted obligation or documented operational concern through its mandate, service and financial perimeter, baseline, feasible alternative, evidence, finding, authorized decision, implementation and observed result. A completed document, software test or research-reference adoption does not establish UN authorization or operational implementation.

## Scope and cutoff

Each portfolio release must freeze a scope record before calculating completion measures. It must identify the review cutoff, instrument population, entities, funding streams, periods, included service families, priority interventions, exclusions and reasons, and accountable release owner. Publication date and underlying observation date must be recorded separately. Later changes create a new scope version and explain denominator changes; difficult or adverse cases cannot be silently removed.

The intended entity perimeter includes active peacekeeping operations; transitions, closures and liquidation with residual liabilities; DPO and relevant DPPA functions; relevant SPM interfaces; Support Account units; RSCE, UNLB and other material shared-service providers. Presence in a register counts as identity coverage only. Detailed financial, operational and reform coverage must be reported separately.

The UNGA80/81 population comprises relevant instruments published or adopted by the cutoff under documented inclusion rules. Adopted obligations, committee action, ACABQ advice, SG proposals and management commitments remain distinct. Session-81 numbering does not establish adoption. Missing implementation evidence does not establish noncompliance. Historical evidence already retained in this repository remains subject to its original scope and limitations.

## Required capabilities

| Capability | Observable completion evidence |
|---|---|
| Authoritative reform baseline | Source-linked obligation register with instrument status, operative anchors, responsible function, deadline/review cycle, implementation evidence and unresolved gaps. |
| Canonical analytical perimeter | Stable entity and intervention IDs; financing periods, units, hierarchy, fund and internal/external relationships; explicit exclusions. |
| Testable interventions | Each active case names an exact service, process, asset, contract, post group or transaction, the proposed change, outputs, cost objects, evidence requirements and nonadditivity group. Umbrella investigations carry no benefit totals. |
| Operational verification | Authorized observations establish what work occurred, its volume and quality, retained and transferred work, and feasibility of the alternative. |
| Defensible financial treatment | Same-scope counterfactual, unique cost objects, transition and continuing costs, uncertainty and incremental treatment support each monetary claim. |
| Protected capability | Prospective mandate, safety, readiness, quality, timeliness and transition thresholds govern advancement and stopping. |
| Accountable decisions | Proposed owner roles and evidence custodians are distinguished from accepted accountable owners; implementation requires documented acceptance and applicable authority. |
| Maintainable evidence | Canonical data, producing code, lineage, evidence dates, preserved releases, structural validation and separate substantive-review receipts support regeneration. |

## Completion gates

All conditions within a gate are mandatory unless a specific item is justified as inapplicable in its acceptance receipt. An unmet condition remains open; it cannot be waived by a favorable financial result.

### Gate 1 — Foundation complete

1. The scope, cutoff, priority population and exclusion log are frozen and versioned.
2. All entities in scope have canonical IDs, lifecycle status, mandate links and financing perimeters. All material provider/client inclusion relationships are recorded.
3. All in-scope reform instruments are classified by authority and linked to authoritative sources. There are no unresolved critical or high-severity authority errors in release claims.
4. Every active intervention has exact grain, proposed owner role, custodian role, evidence plan, nonadditivity group and affected outputs. Naming a role does not imply institutional acceptance.
5. All published headline totals reconcile to their controlling perimeter with exclusions and discrepancies visible.
6. Current and historical artifacts are designated, and known contradictory current assertions are resolved. The historical V11 first-pass result and current V13 adoption can coexist without conflation; the course watch remains paused under the existing instruction.

### Gate 2 — Validation system complete

1. All priority interventions have a standard case dossier containing the decision question, authority, current arrangement, alternative, baseline, evidence request, counterfactual, costs, outputs, safeguards and closure rule.
2. Field-level evidence contracts cover the records required by those interventions. Collection burden and missing-data dispositions are explicit.
3. Secure evidence handling, minimum disclosure and public-repository exclusions are documented and checked.
4. Case-specific observation periods, comparability tests, thresholds and stopping rules are set before outcome review. Where baseline evidence cannot justify a threshold, a baseline-measurement task remains open.
5. Synthetic and public-only rehearsals demonstrate positive, adverse, null, transferred-cost and incomplete-evidence handling. Rehearsals cannot certify operational findings.
6. Every unavailable mandatory input produces a gap receipt naming the blocked conclusion, minimum evidence, custodian role and review date.

### Gate 3 — Decision portfolio complete

1. Every priority case has a reproducible, evidence-qualified disposition and a next accountable decision. `CLOSED_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` is an honest administrative closure, but counts separately from substantive adjudication and cannot satisfy the operational-audit target below.
2. At least three bounded operational audits across at least two service types have completed substantive adjudication. Each must establish the actual baseline and test a feasible alternative or establish why it should be retained. Initial targets are RSCE shared-service charging, UNIFIL–UNSCOL asset transfer, and BINUH–UNSOH aviation/support. Any replacement must be prospectively justified using the prioritization method, with the original case and evidence gap retained.
3. At least one materially relevant top-tier case completes financial adjudication: a supported net financial effect or a supported rejection after the same cost and service tests. Materiality is set before selecting the result. If no top-tier case has sufficient evidence, the release reports this gate as blocked rather than manufacturing a financial finding.
4. All tested adverse, null, retained and unsupported claims remain visible. No particular favorable or unfavorable result is required; the method must demonstrably permit rejection.
5. Portfolio amounts reconcile once across parent/child cases, internal charges, shared enablers and mutually exclusive alternatives. Closures and embedded efficiencies remain baseline effects. Uncertainty and excluded amounts remain visible.
6. Every action recommended for operational implementation has an accepted accountable owner, authority path, implementation and measurement plan, prospective guardrails, transition liabilities, and stop/corrective-action rules. Recommendations to investigate or seek evidence instead name a proposed responsible role, minimum evidence and next decision; they do not require operational implementation approval merely to prepare research. An evidence request or project recommendation alone is not institutional approval.
7. A reviewer other than the case author examines every material recommendation's sources, accounting, alternative explanations and conclusion support. Agent review is identified as such and cannot be presented as independent institutional review.

### Gate 4 — Realization demonstrated or no-change decision completed

Where an intervention is approved, realization requires implementation evidence, reconciled actual cost or observed capacity redeployment, the full predeclared observation period, receiving-entity and residual costs, satisfied operational safeguards, owner acceptance and a review receipt. Forecast, approved and realized amounts remain separate.

If substantive adjudications support no change, the portfolio may instead complete a documented no-change decision with accountable acceptance and a reassessment trigger. This is methodological completion with no realized benefit claimed. Evidence-unavailable closures alone cannot satisfy this gate. If recommendations await authority or evidence, the portfolio remains decision-ready or blocked rather than fully realized.

## Portfolio measures

| Measure | Definition | Target and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Reform evidence coverage | Adopted obligations with source anchor, responsible function, due/review date, evidence status and next action divided by all adopted obligations in the frozen scope. | 100%; reported completeness is not a compliance rate. Evidence-missing and not-yet-due obligations remain visible. |
| Priority disposition coverage | Priority interventions with a recorded evidence-qualified disposition divided by the frozen priority population. | 100% for Gate 3; separately show substantive adjudications, insufficient-evidence closures, and pending authority. A high closure rate cannot substitute for evidence. |
| Substantive operational adjudications | Count of priority interventions with observed operational baselines and completed alternative/retain tests. | At least three across two service types for Gate 3; list cases and scopes. |
| Verified benefit realization | Observed net benefit by claim class, period, nonadditivity group and accepted owner. | No ex ante dollar target. Cash savings, recovery, cost avoidance, capacity and control improvement are reported separately. Unknown is distinct from zero. |

Driver measures include usable evidence received, age of blocking requests, complete case dossiers and implemented cases with current monitoring. Denominators, exclusions and evidence dates accompany all rates. The project must not claim that a percentage of budgets represented in a register is the percentage assessed for waste.

## Release and operating guardrails

Savings language is permitted only at `FINANCIALLY_VALIDATED` or later, supported by the financial evidence gate. Realized totals include only observed benefits meeting realization requirements. Financial benefit cannot override a material mandate, safety, readiness or quality failure. Required normal operations continue through approved fallback arrangements if an evaluation stops.

Release acceptance requires zero unsupported savings claims, zero known double-counted amounts, zero unresolved critical current-status contradictions, and zero restricted source records disclosed publicly. Structural CI must pass from a clean checkout and current public artifacts must regenerate reproducibly. Passing CI is reported separately from substantive source, legal, operational and financial review. Historical sources, V8–V13 controls and prior results remain preserved.

This standard specifies the intended future portfolio; it does not declare any completion gate achieved, certify savings, activate the reporting pilots, or authorize external evidence requests. Existing SP08P1 and SP09P1 live gates remain closed until their applicable requirements are independently met.
